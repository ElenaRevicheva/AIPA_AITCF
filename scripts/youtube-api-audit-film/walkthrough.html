Not five tips. Every check.

What we actually saw on the page, why it matters, and how to fix the ones that fail.

robots.txt allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBotPass
Why it matters: if the crawler is blocked, the model never sees you — ranking on Google cannot save the citation.
llms.txt presentFail
Why it matters: this is the file answer engines look for when they want your official summary.
Fix: publish /llms.txt at the site root naming who you are, what you offer, and the canonical URLs to quote.
JSON-LD Organization / Person identityWarn
Why it matters: machines need a named entity, not just a pretty homepage, before they will attribute an answer to you.
Fix: add Organization JSON-LD with name, url, sameAs (LinkedIn, Wikipedia, Crunchbase).
Question-style headings (FAQ signal)Fail
Why it matters: answer engines match questions to question-shaped content.
Fix: add an FAQ whose H2/H3s are the literal questions customers ask.
The 34 signals · four categories

What it checks

The same four weights that build the score.

AI Crawler Access

Can GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended actually reach your pages? robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap, indexability.

Structured Data (GEO)

Can machines understand what you are? JSON-LD schema, Organization/Person identity, Open Graph, canonical, meta.

Answer-Readiness (AEO)

Will an answer engine quote you? Titles, single H1, question-style headings, content depth, extractable lists and facts.

Technical Foundation

HTTPS, speed, mobile viewport, and content in raw HTML that JS-only crawlers can see.
