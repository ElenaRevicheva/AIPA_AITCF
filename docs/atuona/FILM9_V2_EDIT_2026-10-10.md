## 2. Decisions I made (hers to overturn)

- **Voice OFF.** She put the voice on hold on 8 Oct ("Stop with this for a while"), and the compile stretches every shot
  to its voice line — a shot cannot be "much shorter" while the line runs. Without voice, every shot runs its **planned
  edit length** (the 4:30 plan's seconds), the stanzas stay on screen as text. Switch: `FILM9_NO_VO=1`.
- **"Much shorter" = 3.8 s, "shorter" = 5.0 s** (never above 0.8 × planned; 10b → 3.6 s). The 1.3 s dissolves stay.
- **"Make a (short) glitch" = the shot becomes a Crimson flash** of its approved still (1.2 s; "short" 0.9 s) — she had a
  separate word, "removed", for shots she wanted gone, so these keep their image at flash length. A flash carries no
  stanza (M4, 19, 20, 20b, 35 lose theirs). If she meant "keep the shot and add a flash", it is a one-line change per shot.
- **The four new flashes sit beside the image each echoes** (table above). The three flashes she approved on 7 Oct keep
  their places (G7 after 7m now that 7 is gone; G8_clean after 11; G6s after 14).
- **Music:** the deep-house bed she called ugly is the only track; the film is delivered **with it and without it**
  (same picture), so she can judge the cut either way. Nothing new was generated; $0 spent.
- **Shot 35's flash still** is `k36_double.jpg` — the same double exposure of k36 the preview showed, rendered as a still.
- **Not placed:** her folder `02_APPROVED/09.10.2026 images additionally approved/` (24 stills: G2, G7s, G8s, M3, M5q, M5s,
  k01b, k02, k06, k06_1, k07, k07m, k11, k11_real, k17b, k17b.v1, k20_real, k20b_real, k24rs, k26_real, k27_real, k30i,
  k37.v1, kira_jungle_h) and `09.10.2026 videos additionally approved/` (s13 luma + wan, v24Lb, v25, vM1, vM2, vM3, vM5)
