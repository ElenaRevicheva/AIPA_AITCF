## Shots

### M1 — Another lot
- **Line:** Silent text: "He holds her waist like she's another lot with a pre-sale estimate." (#082)
- **Shows:** Copper afternoon jetty: the model steps off the seaplane, her fiancé's palm flat on her waist; her shadow is a thin bronze sculpture's. Kira watches from behind.
- **Surreal:** Mila's long shadow on the planks is the shadow of a thread-thin, rough bronze sculpture of a walking figure, while everyone else casts a human shadow. Her 'legs like Giacometti sculptures' (#082) taken literally: she arrives as a collected object, a lot. It is held rigid in the wind and nobody notices. It also rhymes with the red dog that casts no shadow (17).

### M2 — Knows this pain by name
- **Line:** Silent text, two cards: "Kira stands slightly behind, watching." / "Knows this pain by name. / Knows how it smells in the morning." (#082)
- **Shows:** Kira close, still, recognising; a band of dawn light lies across her eyes in copper afternoon light. Behind her, soft, he closes his fingers on Mila's wrist.
- **Surreal:** A narrow band of cool dawn light lies across Kira's eyes in the middle of a copper late afternoon, and nothing in the scene could cast it. 'Knows how it smells in the morning' becomes a morning only she is standing in.

### M3 — Cracks by the eyes
- **Line:** Silent text: "M. — mask too tight, cracks by the eyes." (#085) · Sound, no text: the valley church bell strikes three times ("Somewhere below, in the valley, the Catholic church bell struck three times", #085)
- **Shows:** Restaurant at sunset: Mila in profile, white dress, fine old-painting cracks at the corners of her eyes. Jean-Marc studies her like a forgery. A bell strikes three.
- **Surreal:** Old-master varnish craquelure at the outer corners of a living woman's eyes, dry and faintly golden, while the rest of her skin is flawless. Ule's note 'mask too tight, cracks by the eyes' and Jean 'examining a forgery — too perfect to be real' (#085) made literal: the appraiser is looking at a face that has aged like a canvas.

### M4 — A drowning man
- **Line:** Silent text: "Jean reached for her wrist — the movement of a drowning man grabbing at air." (#085; a contiguous clause of "Mila was already rising, brushing sand from her dress, when Jean reached for her wrist…")
- **Shows:** First star over black sand: barefoot Mila rises and brushes her white dress; ultramarine pigment pours off it instead of sand. His hand closes on air.
- **Surreal:** The sand she brushes off her white dress falls as pure ultramarine pigment, the blue 'too expensive, too pure, undiluted by turpentine' (#083), and drifts onto black sand around her white feet. Nobody looks at it. The colour of absence arrives at the moment she leaves.

### M5 — Ultramarine absence
- **Line:** Silent text: "the color of absence, color of a woman who exists only as reflection of someone else's desire." (#083; a contiguous clause of "…I think Renoir really never saw this color — the color of absence…")
- **Shows:** Night: the sofa is empty, sandals kicked off; in the dark window Mila still sits there barefoot in white, looking back. Kira's shoulder at the edge, watching.
- **Surreal:** The sofa is empty, with a dent in the cushion and the sandals kicked off, yet in the dark window Mila still sits on it barefoot in white, looking back. She 'exists only as reflection of someone else's desire' (#083), the 'ghost' of #083. It is the inverse of the approved 'in a mirror she is alone'.
