## What the film is

**A contract of silence, broken twice — first by a lie, then by a name.**

Kira, a hired "hunting dog" with a perfect eye for art, signs a contract whose one clause is silence, with a man who
collects what cannot be bought. In Atuona, hunting a Gauguin that was never on canvas, she finds the thing — and lies
about it. That lie is the first sound a character makes in the film. The second is his name. Between them, the passion
is all in what the contract forbids: a frost-melted kiss on glass, a reflection that undresses while she stands still, a
necklace paid as an advance and asked for again, two people in a cage of shutter-light staring each other down without
blinking, Gauguin's carved lines laid on living skin, two fingers on a mouth that must not speak. It ends not in a
parting but in a fork — two versions of the same woman — and in her own verse: nobody's wife. Atuona.

**Why it isn't soap:** the contract is for *silence*, not sex; Kira announces at signing that she is a lesbian ("Please
consider this for possible group activities") and the night that follows is "not about her sexual preferences" —
recognition, not seduction; the man cries before the woman does; her strength is sobriety held "without blinking"; and
the climax is a password, not a kiss.

## Laws (each one is hers)

1. **The Contract.** "The main and only thing required of you is to create and fiercely protect my silence." (#053)
   Silence in her own definition: "silence is not the absence of sound. / It is when both know what to say and choose not
   to." (#098). No dialogue on camera; lines appear as silent text. **Two words are spoken in the whole film**, both off
   the speaker's face (no lip-sync): Kira's lie, "No. Nothing." (#093), and Kira's truth, "Ule." (#098).
2. **Two voices.** Kira's inner voice = her LITPROM verse. The island and the novel = her ATUONA lines as silent text.
3. **Three notes, never four.** "Three notes, repeat. Three notes, silence." (#088). The cell is never completed — that is
   its allowance. The children's sea game (#004) never reaches "three — freeze!": she refuses the freeze; at the climax
   the freeze happens to *him* — "Ule freezes." (#098).
4. **The body stays. The light moves.** (#081) — bodies near-still, light travelling over them. It is her line, it keeps
   every frame below the explicit line, and it cures AI's over-fast motion.
5. **A color that doesn't exist.** (#087) — broken white ("Blanc cassé… Broken white.", #090) → blue-white North → honey
   → copper → crimson → lilac dawn → black sand that will not whiten (#095). One shadowless red dog.
6. **Multidisciplinary by construction:** her verse (LITPROM), her novel (ATUONA), her glossy column (in chalk on
   asphalt, #050), her code (`// paradise.js`, #064), her auction house and NFT prophecy (Pastorales; «Suum cuique
   размажет все сказки, / Что AI нарисует в блокчейн.», #003), fashion as language, perfume as touch, Gauguin's carving
   as her carving, the ukulele cell, her glitch inserts.
7. **Close to it, never explicit.** «Что, сползая до нитки, не голая» (#030) — bare shoulders, back, hands, mouths, wet
   silk, light; nothing frontal. Niio's Terms: nudity may be present, explicit material is banned.
