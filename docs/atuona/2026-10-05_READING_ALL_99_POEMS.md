## What the two chapters are, read together

**LITPROM is the voice before the island.** Russian, winter, raw and profane, written 2019–2025. Friends under crosses,
mother, father, a daughter, the grandmother who no longer comes in dreams, the pistol in the drawer and «Вечной жизни
обратный билет», the bottle said goodbye to ("Just for ODAT"), Brodsky's hills. A woman who survives by will, and is
funny and tender while doing it. Underground culture in the literal sense.

**ATUONA is the same woman given a second name — Kira — and sent to the edge of the world** to find a painting that does
not exist, *«Атуона — Рай на Земле»*. It is a novel in fragments: Kira (34, sober, the Beast kept in a cage for two,
her mother's suicide and the yellow lilies that keep arriving) and Ule (47, Norwegian, a Russian mother buried in Atuona
next to Gauguin), bound by a contract of **silence**. The island is not decor: Traitor's Bay, Calvaire with its
frangipani, Brel's grave, Gauguin's stone guarded by a copy of Oviri, black sand the Pacific cannot bleach, tikis
"overgrown with moss the color of old blood", an island that "doesn't accept guests. It eats them." The search ends in
the realisation that the painting was never on canvas. Woven through it, a third voice — Paradise.js, the AI co-author
in Panama, 2025.

**The bridge is the name itself.** LITPROM **#003 is titled «Atuona»** and ends with the woman naming herself:
«Я останусь: ничьей женой, / Атуоной, горбатой старушкой / В целом с Богом и чуть - с Сатаной.» And ATUONA #051 opens
with Charles Morice's line in which Atuona is a *she*: "The gods have died, and with their death Atuona is dying."
**Atuona is not only the place on the map. It is the woman who stays.** That is the film.

## The film ATUONA — the spine that comes out of the reading

Not yet a plan (that follows, with cost) — the shape the poems themselves ask for, mostly from never-filmed texts:

1. **Russia, winter (LITPROM).** Kissing the ice on the glass (#044). Rowan and a sunset in ash (#042). «Море волнуется раз» (#004).
2. **Departure.** The corset laced; yellow lilies; "One day I'll drown / In cool silence" (#054 — her verse in RU and EN).
3. **Arrival (ATUONA).** "Morning in Atuona doesn't begin with light. It begins with scent." (#068). Traitor's Bay, Calvaire,
   the stone and Oviri (#057). "Atuona morning, thick as honey. The air tastes of frangipani and rust." (#078).
4. **The search, in silence.** "silence isn't absence. It's presence." (#089). The black hibiscus — "But for now – it's
   beautiful." (#067). "ATUONA breathes a color that doesn't exist." (#087). "Pain didn't leave — it became part of the light." (#085).
5. **The turn.** "paradise isn't a place…" (#077) — the painting is not on canvas.
6. **Coda.** Panama rain — «Ливни, ливни, ливни в Панама сити...» … «Тут Сердце - Театр без зрителей.» (#046) → the title stanza,
   **«Я останусь: ничьей женой, / Атуоной…»** (#003).

Never: #033 (her daughter) unless she chooses it; #098 (explicit). Morice's line (#051) is his, not hers — epigraph only, credited.
