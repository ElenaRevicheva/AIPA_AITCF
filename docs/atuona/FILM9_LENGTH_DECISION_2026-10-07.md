# Film #9 — what length? (research, 7 Oct 2026)

Elena's question: 6 min, shorter or longer — for festivals and Niio.

## What the sources say (read 7 Oct 2026)

| Source | Length fact |
|---|---|
| Niio Open Call (niio.com/site/niio-open-call/) | **No length rule.** Asks for "Video Art / Animation (HD/4K Single Channel)", a high-resolution file + full artwork description. |
| Runway AI Film Festival 2026 (aif.runwayml.com) | Films must be **3–15 min**. |
| Stephen Follows, 13,038 festival regulations | **Under 10 min = eligible at 90%+ of festivals**; 10–20 min ≈ 56%; eligibility stays above 85% until ~15 min, then drops sharply. |
| Short of the Week (shortoftheweek.com) | Average featured short ≈ 13 min; Sundance 2022 avg 13:22, SXSW 12:46; Cannes shorts max 15 min. |
| FilmDaft (secondary, citing Sundance) | ≤10 min "doubles the chance" of being programmed — second-hand, not verified at source. |

## Reading

- 4:30 and 6:00 are **both** inside every window found: over Runway's 3-min minimum, under the 10-min line where
  eligibility is 90%+, and within Cannes' 15-min cap.
- The 13-min averages are for live-action narrative shorts. This film is a poetic text/voice piece built from AI motion,
  where every extra shot is another chance for a face to drift — no source says longer helps it.
- Niio gives no length rule; it asks for HD/4K. The 4K engine (Kling O3 4K) matches that ask.
- Nothing found supports going **longer** than ~6 min for this film.

## Recommendation

**The 4:30 cut.** The 8 shots it drops (3, 4, 8, 12, 16a, 23, 29, 33) are exactly the ones with no frame, so it costs the
least, keeps every never-cut shot, and loses no festival or Niio eligibility. Still to make before motion: 15, 31, the
redesign of 30 (and 10a/10b if they stay in).
