## Three decisions for Elena — too many words for the shot

A viewer reads about 3 words a second. These three shots carry more than that:

1. **Shot 25 (63 words in 10 s).** The #042 voice in the scenario is eight lines. **Recommended:** the two lines above
   ("Well then, you will be a guest this autumn / in My Country." — the heart of the shot: the Kingdom is hers, he is the
   guest). Alternative: all eight lines, with the shot held about 22 s (+12 s on the film).
2. **Shot 26 (39 words in 10 s).** **Recommended:** hold the shot 13 s (slow the clip ×1.3) — no words cut, +3 s.
3. **Shot 28 (40 words in 10 s).** **Recommended:** hold the shot 13.5 s — no words cut, +3.5 s.

With the recommended choices the film runs about 4:40 before the edit trims silent beats.

The other 🟠/✅ shots sit between 2 and 2.9 words a second — readable.
