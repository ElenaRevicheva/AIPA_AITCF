## The film in one paragraph

A woman signs a contract of silence with a man who collects what cannot be bought, and follows him to the village where
Gauguin died, to find a painting that was never on canvas. The film obeys her contract: **no one speaks on camera.**
Her thoughts are her verse; the island and the novel answer in silent text; he never says a word — he "learned how to
shut up". The passion is all in what the contract forbids: breath at the nape, a necklace clasped twice, a finger along
an old scar, a palm pressed to a throat, moonlight laying Gauguin's carved lines across living skin. In the last minutes
she breaks the contract with one spoken word — his name — and the film learns what her vault already knew: *"The
painting is not lost. It was never on canvas." "Then we are already inside it."* She stays nobody's wife. She stays
Atuona.

## The film's laws (each one is hers)

1. **The Contract.** "The main and only thing required of you is to create and fiercely protect my silence." (#053) No
   dialogue. One spoken word in the whole film — "Ule." (#098), near the end.
2. **Two voices only.** Kira's inner voice = her poems (LITPROM, translated). The island / the novel = her ATUONA lines as
   silent on-screen text, in her own English.
3. **The three notes.** "Three notes, repeat. Three notes, silence." (#088) — the score's cell, from the first second to
   the last. The missing "three" of the sea game (#004) is the same silence.
4. **Recognition, not attraction.** "not attraction, something more dangerous. Recognition." (#077) / "Not from
   pleasure. / From recognition." (#098) — the erotic register: faces that see through, bodies that answer.
5. **A colour that doesn't exist.** "ATUONA breathes a color that doesn't exist." (#087) — blue-white North → honey →
   copper → crimson; one shadowless red dog walks through it.
6. **Multidisciplinary by construction:** her poems (both chapters), her prose novel, her glossy column, her code
   (`paradise.js`), her auction house (Pastorales), fashion as language, perfume as touch, Gauguin's carvings as her
   carving, her gallery's arthouse glitch inserts (~1.2 s, RGB split, from her own stills and keyframes).
7. **Never explicit.** Bare back, shoulders, hands, mouths at the nape and the throat, wet silk, sheets, shadows of carved
   lines on skin. Nothing frontal, nothing graphic. Niio's Terms: nudity may be present, explicit material is banned.

## I · THE CONTRACT — the North, blue-white, locked-off

**1. The empty wall.** A white gallery at night, no paintings — Pastorales before its first lot. One empty wall lit like a
canvas. Kira in a check suit, tie, lilac silk blouse (#053), facing the wall. Ule behind her in tweed, so close the
fabric of her jacket stirs.
- Text: **"The main and only thing required of you is to create and fiercely protect my silence."** (#053)
- Text: **"He does not touch her. He simply stands close enough that she feels the heat of his body through the
  fabric."** (#097)
- Sound: the room's hum; three notes, very far.

**2. The ice.** A frosted window in the same gallery, night outside. She leans in and presses her lips to the frost; a
clear oval melts; her breath fogs it again.
- Kira's voice (#044): «Поцелуя не жду. Губами / Расцелую я лед на стеклах.» — *draft:* "I don't wait for a kiss. With my
  lips / I'll kiss the ice on the glass."

**3. Aces and enemies.** A small card table between them. His long fingers lay down an ace. Hers lay down a card face
down, and leave it.
- Kira's voice (#044): «Ты - фартовый. Ходи тузами. / Я - врагами. Пускай не мешают.» — *draft:* "You're lucky. Play your
  aces. / I'll play enemies. Let them not stand in the way."

*Glitch: the frost oval.*

## II · THE CROSSING — night water

**4. The leaf.** Black air. One burnt leaf spirals *upward*, lit from inside like a plane window. (#055: "as casually as
a burnt-out soul in the cells of a leaf torn from its branch – smoothly spiraling upward.")
- No words. The three notes, closer.

**5. The game without "three".** From above: a black sea under a low moon; Kira swims away on the surface in a dark slip,
bare shoulders, wet strap.
- Kira's voice (#004): «Море волнуется раз. / Море волнуется два.» — *draft:* "The sea is restless — one. / The sea is
  restless — two."
- Then **1.2 s of absolute silence** — the "three" that never comes — over an RGB-split still of her eye.
- Kira's voice (#004): «Наживка с крючка сорвалась. / Я шлепнулась и уплыла.» — *draft:* "The bait tore loose from the
  hook. / I flopped down and swam away."

**6. Scales and wings.** Two images cut against each other. Silver fish scales sliding off a man's wet shoulder in
moonlight, like sequins coming loose. White feathers drifting into a pillowcase held open by her hands.
- Kira's voice (#036): «Я тебя крылатая, дождусь, / перьями подушку набиваю. / Зря ты снова сбросил чешую / там, где я
  тихонечко взлетаю.» — *draft:* "Winged, I will wait for you, / stuffing a pillow with feathers. / In vain you shed your
  scales again / where I am quietly taking off."

*Glitch: one feather on black water.*

## IV · THE DRESS — night, storm, copper

**12. THAT dress.** A restaurant on the island in the first gust of a tropical storm. Kira walks in in the Jungle Dress,
hair up. Every head turns except his.
- Text: **"Kira walked into the restaurant in THAT dress."** (#058)

**13. The clasp.** He stands behind her and closes a waterfall of diamonds and emeralds around her neck — the click

**14. Again.** After the storm, in an armchair, she takes it off — then holds it out to him. He fastens it again, slowly,
and lowers his face to the nape of her neck. He breathes her in.
- Text: **"asked him to fasten the Graff on her again."** (#060)
- Text: **"lifting the Kilian veil, breathing in her skin, All of her..."** (#060)

**15. On the Rocks.** A still life in candlelight: the necklace laid on the table in a semicircle; inside it, a glass of

## V · THE HUNT — noon, black sand, the gap

**16. The dog with no shadow.** Hard white noon on black sand. Everything casts a black shadow — palms, a rock, Kira.
A red dog trots past her and casts none. She follows it.
- Text: **"Fact: dogs aren't red. / Fact: it has no shadow."** (#087)

**17. The carvings.** Inside the house, carved wooden lintels in raking light: figures and words cut deep into the wood,
seen only in fragments, never legible, never explicit. Her fingertips run along the grooves.
- Text: **"Words that could never be printed. / Words that still haven't been forgiven."** (#095)

**18. Her carving.** 4 a.m. The same groove pattern dissolves into lines of code reflected on her face from a laptop.
- On screen, as her code: `if (darkness === true) { return stars.map(pain => light) }` (#064)
- Text: **"This is her carving. / Not on breadfruit wood / but in TypeScript. / Same rage. / Different medium."** (#095)

**19. The auction without a painting.** A dark room, an easel with an empty gilt frame. White paddles rise in the
darkness. Ule bids without a sound. In the frame, for a moment, only Kira's eyes.
- Text: **"Paddles rise like white birds."** (#067)
- Text (his thought): **"Her eyes are both the tile and the master's creation,"** / **"I'd buy them from her for my
  collection."** (#086)
- Sound: the hammer — **"The hammer falls like a gunshot."** (#067)

**20. My Country.** She turns from the empty frame to him, takes the necklace off and closes it around *his* wrist. Night

- Kira's voice (#042): «Что ж, будешь гостем этой осенью / В Моей Стране. / Вмиг ночь истомная расхристана / Вся, догола. /
  Казненным будешь ты без милости, / Сгоришь до тла!..» — *draft:* "Well then, you'll be a guest this autumn / in My

## VI · THE BREAK OF SILENCE — night, crimson

**21. Night.** The house after rain; wet hibiscus; the dark room.
- Text: **"Night did not ask permission. / It simply stopped pretending."** (#096)

**22. Carved light.** Moonlight through a carved window falls on his chest in the shapes of the lintel; the grooves of

- Text: **"Moonlight from the Maison du Jouir window falls across his chest exactly along the lines Gauguin once carved
  into the lintel."** / **"Now those lines live on living skin."** (#098)

**23. Pulse.** Her palm flat on his chest, rising with his breath.
- Text: **"Under the skin — steady pulse. / Not consensus. / Just proof that the chain still holds."** (#096)

**24. The line.** His finger along her spine — the old scar — slowly.
- Text: **"Ule runs a finger down her spine. / Not down. / Not up. / Exactly along the line"** (#096)
- Text: **"As if the body itself had surrendered the private key."** (#096)

**25. The second.** Their faces a breath apart. Everything stops — picture held, score at absolute zero, the same silence
as the missing "three".
- Text: **"Between compile and run / there's a second. / Pure. / Naked."** (#074)

**26. Proof of work.** Her hands on a bamboo mat, fingers closing; the knots press into her palms. His hand closes over
hers.
- Text: **"Bamboo nodes bite her palms like the body wants to leave proof-of-work on this world."** (#098)

**27. The word.** Her face, close. For the first time in the film a voice is heard on camera — hers, barely:
- Spoken: **"Ule."** (#098)
- Text: **"Not a request. / A fork."** (#098)
- His palm takes hers and presses it to his throat. Text: **"Ule takes her palm and presses it to his throat. / To the
  place where life beats."** (#096)
- Sound: the three notes return — and this time a fourth. Text: **"Not a lullaby. / Not a song. / Allowance."** (#088)

*Glitch: carved shadows on skin.*
