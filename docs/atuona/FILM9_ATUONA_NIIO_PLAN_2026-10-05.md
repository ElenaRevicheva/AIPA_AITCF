## The idea, in one paragraph

Atuona is not only the village on Hiva Oa. In LITPROM #003 — her poem titled «Atuona» — the woman names herself:
«Я останусь: ничьей женой, / Атуоной…». In the ATUONA chapter, Morice's line makes Atuona a *she*. So the film is a
crossing — from a frozen northern window to the black sand above Traitor's Bay — in which a woman becomes Atuona.
**One strict formal rule: the woman has a voice (her LITPROM lines, translated); the island answers only in silent
on-screen text (her ATUONA lines, in her own English).** The score rises into each silence.
