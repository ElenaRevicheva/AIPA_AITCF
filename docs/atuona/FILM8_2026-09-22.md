## 4. Engine choice — measured on one shot

shots, Grok Imagine 1.5 for wide/landscape**.

## 5. The line — what "adult" means here

Implied, not explicit: wet silk, bare backs and shoulders, bodies in water, light on skin, raw faces. **A refusal is final
for that prompt** — re-phrase it into that register, never re-route it to a more permissive model or strip the reference to
get it through (tried once for a nude keyframe; the permission gate blocked it as weakening a safety control, and the route
was removed from the tool).
