# What Leaply actually is — and how its method lives inside EspaLuz (3 Nov 2026)

Elena sent getleaply.com/en/kds on 19 Oct 2026 ("brain activation exercises for kids") and asked for its practices
inside Pere & Titi. Read 3 Nov 2026 from Leaply's own pages and third-party descriptions. The website itself is a quiz
funnel (getleaply.com/en/kds asks only "Who are you raising? Boy / Girl"; theleaply.com: "Playful daily brain breaks to
help kids focus, self-regulate, and reset after screen time"); the plan content sits behind the quiz and paywall.

## What Leaply does (sources below)
- **Mainly an adult wellbeing programme** (Momentumix Ltd; App Store "Leaply — Habits that fit your life", 4.7★, 3.8K
  ratings, "140K+ people"): short daily practices (3–10 min per a reviewer; 5 to 20+ min chosen in the quiz) — breathing
  paced by **bell cues** for in/out, **self-massage**, **body scans**, **side-alternating movement sequences** with verbal
  cues, acupressure; weekly themes (sleep, energy, cognition) that "progress from basic to more sophisticated"; stress
  tracking. Healthify NZ (psychologist review, Aug 2025, 4★): "a good programme teaching evidence-based skills", safety
  concerns "Nil".
- **Kids plan "Brain Activation Exercises for Kids"**: "short playful exercises" for "focus, attention, and emotional
  regulation", "described visually, which keeps screen time minimal", done "in a few focused minutes". Every session
  has one flow: **a short framing → the exercise with its time stated → a brief wrap-up**. No named kids exercises are
  published. A parent takes the quiz; the plan is for the parent to run.
- Sources are partly advertiser-adjacent review sites — treated as descriptions, never quoted as evidence of effect.

## What we never say
No health, nerve or brain claims ("activates the vagus nerve", "boosts focus" etc.) — they are plain games and calm
moments. No "Leaply" name in anything a family sees.
