**Heading:** EspaLuz: your family's Spanish-English companion on WhatsApp, Telegram and the web

1. **Talk or type, in your own words**
   - Write or send a voice note in English, Spanish or Russian, the way you would to a friend.
   - Every answer comes back in Spanish and English, as text and as voice.
   - Children ask in their own words; nobody has to sound perfect.
   - Practise in the time you already have: in the car, in the kitchen, between errands.
2. **Your day becomes the lesson**
   - Send a real question: a shopping list, a note from school, "where is the nearest pharmacy?"
   - You get the phrase you need in both languages, ready to use today.
   - New words come from your life, not from a textbook chapter.
3. **Snap a photo, understand it**
   - Menus, street signs, forms, your child's homework.
   - EspaLuz explains what it says and what it means where you live.
4. **Calm help on hard days**
   - Moving is stressful. Answers stay short, calm and practical.
   - Shy children practise through play with Pere and Titi, at their own pace.
5. **One companion for the whole family**
   - Knows who is who: a parent asking about work phrases, a child practising colours, a grandparent learning greetings.
   - Each person gets answers at their own level.
   - One family memory, the same on WhatsApp, Telegram and the web.
6. **Hear her, and on the paid plan, see her**
   - EspaLuz answers by voice; on the paid plan she also replies in a few short videos each month.
