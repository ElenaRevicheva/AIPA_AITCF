## What replika.com actually does (checked live in Chrome, 5 Nov 2026)

- The hero person is a **video, not an animation file**: `hero_video_avatar_1.webm` and `_2.webm`, each **10.04 s**,
  `autoplay muted loop playsInline`, with a still **poster** picture shown until the video loads.
- **Two avatars take turns**, swapped in step with the headline's rotating phrase
  ("The AI friend *to do life / to do side quests / to fall in love / to become yourself* with").
- The person is **full body, standing, idle motion only** (breathing, a look, a small gesture), inside a **frosted-glass
  rounded panel** (~365 × 556 px, about 2:3) on a soft sky picture.
- Two **tilted "stickers" float over the panel**: a chat note ("these reminded me of you") and a shared photo/video
  card. They are separate pictures laid on top, so the text is crisp and never generated by AI.

What we copy: short silent loop, still poster fallback, one clip per idea, real text as stickers outside the video.
What we do not copy: their characters, their sky, their wording.

## How to use the prompts in Grok Imagine

1. Grok → **Imagine** → upload the picture named for that prompt (image-to-video).
2. Paste the prompt. Pick **portrait 2:3** (or 9:16 if 2:3 is not offered) and the **longest length** Grok allows.
3. Generate 2–3 tries and keep the one where the **first and last frame match** (it loops without a jump).
4. Put the files on the Desktop and tell Claude the names — Claude shrinks them, adds the poster, wires them in and
   checks they play on phone and laptop. The video carries no words; text stays on the page.
