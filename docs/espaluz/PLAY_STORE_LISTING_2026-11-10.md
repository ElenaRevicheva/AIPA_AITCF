Expat families & digital nomads in LATAM: a bilingual long-road companion in all your chats - WhatsApp, Telegram,
web - plus a kids classroom.

EspaLuz is not a course and not a tutor. She is a companion for the long road of a new life in a Spanish-speaking
country: from the first months of adapting to the day it starts to feel like home.

She answers in Spanish and English side by side, on the go, in the chats you already use. You can write or speak
to her in Spanish, English or Russian.
The conversation is about your real life, and the Spanish comes with it.

A COMPANION THAT BELONGS TO YOUR FAMILY
- She remembers your family: who you are, your children, where you live or are heading, what worries you and what
  you look forward to.
- Link WhatsApp, Telegram and the app, and she keeps one memory of your family across all three.
- The more you talk, the better she knows you, and her answers fit your family's life, not a textbook.
- She brings your family closer to each other and to your new home. She never pretends to be a person and never
  invents a memory.

BELONGING, NOT ONLY ADAPTING
- Local life, culture, traditions and ways of thinking in 21 Spanish-speaking countries of Latin America, written
  from checked sources.
- The everyday words, customs and small talk that turn a new place into your place.
- When paperwork comes up, take a photo of the letter, form or sign and ask what it means.

FOR THE CHILDREN
- A classroom with two friendly teachers, Pere and Titi, opened by a parent. Its games follow Cambridge Primary
  goals (maths, music, computing, first words).

FOR NOMADS AND TRAVELERS STILL CHOOSING
- Still deciding where to settle? Ask what everyday life is like in each country before you choose.
- Practise real conversations before you need them.
- Speak or type; EspaLuz can answer with a voice.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
No ads. The children's classroom opens only after a parent signs in and agrees. Parents can see and delete what is
kept.

PRICE
Downloading is free and you can try a short chat without an account. Full use needs a subscription after the free
trial; the price is shown before you pay, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

EspaLuz is an AI companion. It is not professional or official advice.
