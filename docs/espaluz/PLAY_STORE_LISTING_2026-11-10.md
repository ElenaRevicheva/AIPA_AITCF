A bilingual companion for expat families and digital nomads in Latin America. Across WhatsApp, Telegram and web, with a dedicated classroom for children.

EspaLuz is not a course or another AI language tutor. She is a companion for the long road of making a new country feel like home.

From your first months of adapting to the day when a foreign place finally becomes your home, EspaLuz accompanies your family through everyday life in Spanish-speaking countries.

She answers in Spanish and English side by side, on the go, in the chats you already use. You can write or speak to her in Spanish, English or Russian. She helps you understand, communicate and gradually feel at home, in the conversations and situations that matter in real life.

ONE COMPANION. YOUR WHOLE FAMILY.
- She remembers your family: your children, your home, your plans, your worries and the things you look forward to.
- One shared family memory. Connect WhatsApp, Telegram and the EspaLuz app and continue the same conversation across them.
- Personal, not textbook-based. The more you talk, the better her answers fit your family's circumstances and everyday life.
- A bridge to your new home. EspaLuz helps your family connect with each other and with the world around you.
- Honest AI. She is built never to pretend to be human and never to invent memories.

BELONGING, NOT ONLY ADAPTING
Life abroad is about much more than paperwork, supermarkets, restaurants and vocabulary.
- Explore local life, culture, traditions and ways of thinking across 21 Spanish-speaking countries in Latin America.
- Learn the everyday expressions, customs and small talk that help you connect with people.
- Understand unfamiliar letters, forms and signs: take a photo and ask EspaLuz what they mean.
- Practise the Spanish you actually need where life happens: at the doctor's, with a landlord or realtor, with your children's teachers or your neighbours.

A CLASSROOM FOR THE LITTLE ONES
A children's learning space, opened by a parent, with two friendly characters: Pere the sloth and Titi the tamarin.
- Playful learning through voice, games and drawing.
- Early maths, music, computing and first phrases, built on international primary school goals (Cambridge Primary and Pre A1 Starters themes, Singapore-style maths).
- The parent opens the classroom and can stay and join in; Pere and Titi notice when a grown-up is talking.

STILL CHOOSING YOUR NEXT COUNTRY?
For digital nomads, relocating families and travelers:
- Ask what everyday life is like in different Spanish-speaking countries before deciding where to settle.
- Practise real-life conversations before you need them.
- Discover local lifestyle and cultural differences.
- Type or speak naturally. EspaLuz answers in text and voice, and subscribers can receive short video replies from her avatar.

DESIGNED WITH FAMILIES IN MIND
- No advertising.
- The children's classroom opens only after a parent signs in and agrees.
- Parents can see what EspaLuz remembers and ask us to delete it.
- A family-oriented experience for learning and everyday communication.

PRICING
Downloading EspaLuz is free. You can try a short conversation without creating an account.

Full access requires a subscription. New subscribers start with a 14-day free trial and are charged only when it ends, unless they cancel before. The price is shown before you subscribe. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.

AN IMPORTANT NOTE
EspaLuz is an AI-powered companion for language learning, cultural discovery and everyday communication. She does not provide professional, medical, legal or official government advice.

A new country takes time to become home. EspaLuz walks the long road with your family.
