## Full description (max 4000)
Expat families & digital nomads in LATAM: a bilingual long-road companion in all your chats - WhatsApp, Telegram,
web - plus a kids classroom inspired by international school programs.

Moving to a Spanish-speaking country with your family? EspaLuz helps you live there in Spanish: at school, at the
doctor, at the bank, with the neighbours.

EspaLuz is an AI companion that answers in Spanish and in your language at the same time, so every reply also
teaches you the words you need for that day.

FOR FAMILIES SETTLING IN
- Tell EspaLuz about your move once: your country, your children, what worries you. It remembers your family and
  answers for your situation, not a textbook one.
- A step-by-step map of the first months in a new country.
- A classroom for children with two friendly teachers, Pere and Titi, opened by a parent. Its games follow
  Cambridge Primary goals (maths, music, computing, first words).
- Take a photo of a letter, a form or a sign and ask what it means and how to answer.
- Local guidance for 21 Spanish-speaking countries in Latin America.

FOR NOMADS AND TRAVELERS
- Practise real conversations before you need them: renting, shopping, asking for help.
- Speak or type; EspaLuz can answer with a voice.

ONE COMPANION EVERYWHERE
Use EspaLuz in the app, on WhatsApp or on Telegram. Link them and EspaLuz keeps one memory of your family across all
three.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
No ads. The children's classroom opens only after a parent signs in and agrees. Parents can see and delete what is kept.

PRICE
Downloading is free and you can try a short chat without an account. Full use needs a subscription after the free
trial; the price is shown before you pay, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

EspaLuz is a language and everyday-life companion. It does not replace a doctor, lawyer or official advice.
