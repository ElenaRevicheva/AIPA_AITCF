# Pro English pronunciation vs competitors (21 Nov 2026)

Question (Elena): do Loora and the other English coaches work the way our new practice loop works?
Sources: public pages found by web search on 21 Nov 2026 (listed at the end). Third-party reviews are marked as such;
nothing here was tested inside the competitors' apps.

## What each one does with pronunciation

| | Score per word | Hear the right word | Say it again, re-scored | Which SOUND is wrong | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Loora** | yes (word breakdown, sentence % e.g. 78%) - app-store screenshots, reviews | yes ("native speaker suggestion") | yes ("practise as many times as you want") - user review | partly ("phonetic feedback on syllables") - one review | inside the conversation: "Tap to fix" on your message |
| **ELSA Speak** | yes, colour-coded words | yes | yes (its core product) | **yes, phoneme level + mouth/tongue tips** | dedicated drills + Speech Analyzer |
| **Praktika** | "feedback button" per line - one affiliate-style review | unclear | unclear | no | conversation; many Trustpilot reviews say pronunciation feedback is weak or missing |
| **Duolingo Max (Video Call)** | no word score in official material | - | - | no | feedback on accuracy/complexity after the call; pronunciation is in separate sound drills |
| **EspaLuz Pro English (after 84ab094)** | yes (Azure, per word, worst 2 under 75) | yes (coach voice, normal + slow) | yes ("53 -> 88", tries saved, counted in weekly progress) | **not yet** | inside the conversation, additive box under the coach answer |

## Verdict

- Our loop is the **Loora pattern**: pronunciation feedback inside a real conversation, the weak words listed, hear them, say
  them again until right. That is the right pattern for our buyer (an adult who needs English for work), and better than
  Duolingo Max and (by its own reviews) Praktika on pronunciation.
- What we lack is **ELSA's layer**: which sound inside the word was wrong, with a tip. ELSA is the pronunciation specialist;
  its reviewers also report mis-grading, so it is not perfect either.
- Our real edge is not pronunciation: it is **one coach and one memory across web, WhatsApp and Telegram**, in the
  apps LATAM professionals already use, included in the $11 family plan. No competitor above works inside WhatsApp.

## Next (additive, same Azure call, no new tool)

1. Sound-level hint: switch the same Azure assessment from `Granularity: Word` to `Phoneme` and show ONE hint per weak
   word ("the 'j' sound in en-gi-neer"). Closes the main gap with ELSA.
2. Optional: replay your own try next to the coach's word (Loora/ELSA both let you compare).
3. Optional: the practice buttons on WhatsApp/Telegram are not possible as buttons; there it would be "send a voice note
   saying just *time*" - the brain's drill can already score it.
