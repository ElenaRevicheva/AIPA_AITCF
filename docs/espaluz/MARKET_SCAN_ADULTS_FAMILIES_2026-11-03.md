## B. What competitors have that EspaLuz lacks

**(i) Cheap to adapt with pieces EspaLuz already has**
1. **Daily streak + reminder** sent by the bot itself on WhatsApp/Telegram ("Day 6 🔥 — one phrase for today").
   Duolingo Q4 2024 filing (via sqmagazine.co.uk): >10M users with 1-year+ streaks; a third of daily users have a Friend Streak.
2. **Family streak**: Friend Streak mapped onto the existing child/Mamá/Papá chat — fits EspaLuz better than Duolingo.
3. **Shareable progress card** rendered from the existing weekly family recap (stars, new phrases, life event handled).
   Duolingo: a stat on the Year-in-Review card "increased overall share rates"; "millions" share it (blog.duolingo.com, Dec 2022; no %).
4. **Referral reward in days**: invites already exist → add "+7 days for both of you" (only once the grant is really coded —
   the false "you BOTH get 1 month FREE" promise was removed 2 Nov for exactly that reason).
5. **Family-plan framing**: the $120/365-day pass ≈ Duolingo Super Family $119.99/yr. Label it "the whole family".
6. **Mascot content**: Pere & Titi carry recurring skits (see D).

**(ii) Rebuild — do not copy:** leagues/leaderboards (need scale), an app-store app, animated video-call avatars,
a large structured curriculum, a human-tutor marketplace.

## C. What EspaLuz has that others do not — overlaps flagged honestly
**Overlaps:** Causa (WhatsApp + voice + 4 regional variants — closest adult match); Memrise MemBot (free, big brand,
WhatsApp, "cultural insights"); other WhatsApp tutors (ChiaChat, Llanai, Parlai, Pal); Talkpal photo description
(a drill, not "explain this document"); relocation AI tools (LatamGuru, MoveSmart, SettleIn, Ask Rudy, Plarelo) do
visas/logistics but no language; Expats Spanish (MX), James Spanish School (ES) = human courses.

**Not found anywhere as one product:** living-in-the-country framing (doctor, school, offices, forms) for 21 countries
+ kids both directions ES↔EN + two-way interpreter + family 3-way chat with parent report + life-event follow-ups,
on WhatsApp + Telegram + web with no install. **The edge is the combination. WhatsApp alone is no longer a moat.**

## D. Organic growth playbook a zero-budget founder can automate
1. **Mascot as recurring character, not ad.** Duolingo formula: entertainment first, trending topics, running storylines,
   answering comments; ~7M TikTok followers, also Shorts + Reels (blog.hubspot.com/marketing/duolingo-unhinged-content;
   campaignlive.com). EspaLuz: "Titi at the pediatrician in Panama", "Pere mixes up *chiva* and *bus*".
2. **Situational phrases beat grammar** (audienceplayer.com): "what to say at the farmacia / school office / Migración".
3. **Word/slang of the day, 30–45 s, on-screen text** (growithwords, fluxnote.io). 21 countries of slang → series
   "Same word, 3 countries".
4. **Struggle comedy and English-for-Spanish-speakers rising; rapid vocab lists fading** (virlo.ai — vendor sample, lower
   confidence). Supports posting in BOTH directions.
5. **Shareable result cards** that families post themselves (the weekly recap image).

Not found: verified organic-growth evidence for Praktika, Speak, Buddy or Lingokids. Speak's creator program is paid.

## E. Pricing — $11 / 30 days
- Below the AI-conversation tier: Duolingo Max $29.99, Langua $19.99–29.99, Speak ≈$18–20, Pimsleur $20.99.
- About equal: Babbel $13.95–15, Busuu/Memrise ≈$14, ELSA $11.99.
- Above: Talkpal from $5.99, Praktika ≈$8, Duolingo Super $6.99–12.99, MemBot free.
- **Position:** an AI-tier tutor for the whole family at a single-user price. Lead with "family" and "it lives in WhatsApp",
  not with price. 14-day trial matches Talkpal.
- ⛔ The "67% monthly retention" line in an old Dev.to post is self-reported and unverified — never reuse it.
