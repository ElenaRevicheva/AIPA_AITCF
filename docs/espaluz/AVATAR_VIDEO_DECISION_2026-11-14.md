## What a user actually gets from a live clip in chat
They asked something, they got the text answer (and voice) at once, then wait ~20 s to watch a face say the same words.
The face adds no new information, the lips are close but not exact (Elena's own judgement 11 Nov, Bear&Fox note), and on
WhatsApp a 20 s wait reads as "the bot is broken". That is why nobody uses it: it is a simulation of presence, not help.
