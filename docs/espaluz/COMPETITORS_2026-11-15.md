## B. What competitors clearly do BETTER than EspaLuz

1. **Course depth and structure.** Babbel, Busuu, Rosetta Stone and Pimsleur have full A1–B2 curricula. Language Lab claims 18,000+ lessons ([language-lab.io](https://language-lab.io/)). EspaLuz has no comparable sequenced adult syllabus.
2. **Polish, brand and trust.** Duolingo is a household name, with daily active users up 29% in Q3 2026 ([stocktitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/duolingo-q3-2026-earnings-daily-users-up-29-percent-max-plan-ends)). Parents trust the owl and its child-safety setup (COPPA child accounts). EspaLuz is unknown.
3. **Speech technology.** Speak, Praktika and Duolingo Video Call (now going into Super at no extra cost) offer real-time voice with pronunciation feedback. ELSA-style phoneme scoring exists elsewhere. EspaLuz's voice is good, but it does not score pronunciation.
4. **Price per person.** Duolingo Super Family costs about $10–12/month for six people ([dealnews](https://www.dealnews.com/features/duolingo/cost/)), which is the same as EspaLuz, from a famous brand. ChatGPT and Gemini are free with memory. Voiczy costs €5–7 for up to 3 kids.
5. **Human teachers.** Preply and italki tutors from $4–17 a lesson can be *local* Panamanians who teach real culture and correct you live. No AI matches that for a nervous adult.
6. **Kids' content volume.** Lingokids, Dinolingo and Rockalingua have years of songs, games and videos, and Voiczy covers ages 3–14. EspaLuz's classroom covers only ages 6–7.
7. **A rival already owns "language for moving abroad".** Language Lab has a Settle Mode, 100 real-life scenes per language and 50 languages. Its depth today is Germany, not LATAM.

## D. Why pay $11/month instead of Duolingo or free ChatGPT?

**3 strongest honest reasons**
1. **It is about settling in, not studying.** Every big app teaches a course. EspaLuz is the only one found that knows *which country* the family lives in, what holidays and customs are coming, and what newcomers there find hard, with checked facts. ChatGPT gives unchecked answers only when asked.
2. **One companion for the whole family, in the chat they already use.** Kids in a voice class, parents on WhatsApp, all sharing one memory ("Alisa had her first school play on 3 Nov"). Competitors sell one account per person, even on family plans.
3. **It prepares them for real life events.** "Tomorrow is the parent meeting" turns into a rehearsal of *that* meeting, in both languages. Generic apps teach generic restaurant dialogues.

**3 strongest objections a parent would raise**
1. **"ChatGPT and Gemini are free and remember me too."** That is true since 2025–26 ([businesstoday](https://businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/chatgpt-app-can-now-remember-more-about-your-conversations-without-a-paid-subscription-535126-2026-06-05), [9to5google](https://9to5google.com/2026/02/26/gemini-past-chats-free/)). EspaLuz must *show* what free AI does not do (checked country facts, family-shared memory, a kids' class), not just say it.
2. **"Duolingo Family costs the same for 6 people, has a real course, and now includes Video Call."** ([dealnews](https://www.dealnews.com/features/duolingo/cost/), [stocktitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/duolingo-q3-2026-earnings-daily-users-up-29-percent-max-plan-ends)) Brand and depth win unless the settling-in value is obvious within the first week.
3. **"Who are you, and is it safe for my child?"** An unknown brand with an AI talking to a 6-year-old raises privacy and trust questions. The kids' range (6–7) is also narrow, so a family with a 10-year-old sees nothing for them.

## E. Cheap ideas to adapt (no new products)

1. **Make the memory visible in the first week (Duolingo's Lily pattern).** Duolingo markets that Lily "brings up your London trip" ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/ai-and-video-call)). EspaLuz could open a chat with a callback to a dated family moment it already stores, and show that moment in onboarding and promos. This is the clearest answer to the "ChatGPT also remembers" objection, because it is *family* memory.
2. **"First 30 days" checklist from existing belonging guides (Language Lab's Settle Mode).** Language Lab sells a first-week checklist (bank, registration, doctor) ([language-lab.io](https://language-lab.io/)). EspaLuz could turn its 21 checked country guides into a per-country checklist of first weeks and first holidays, and tick items as the family reports them. This reuses data it already has.
3. **Show the per-person price (Babbel Group and Duolingo Family pricing).** Competitors sell "up to 6 people". EspaLuz is per family already, so it can say so: "$11/month for the whole family, under $2 per person for a family of six". This removes objection 2 on price.
4. **Free entry on WhatsApp, then a 7-day trial (Parlai, Polyato, Spanish Chat).** Parlai gives one free activity a day ([parlai](https://www.parlai.app/#pricing)). Spanish Chat gives a 7-day trial plus a HOLA promo code ([pricing](https://spanish.chat/pricing)). A limited free daily belonging tip on WhatsApp, with no app install, lowers the "unknown brand" barrier.
5. **Multi-child seats and a weekly parent summary (Voiczy, "up to 3 children").** ([voiczy](https://www.voiczy.com/en/talk-to-ai)) State clearly how many kids one family plan covers, and send the existing parent report as a weekly WhatsApp message instead of only inside the app.
