# EspaLuz: what is the core difference? (13 Nov 2026)

Elena's question: everyone translates, reads photos, hears voices and takes documents. Is our difference memory and a bond
with the user that evolves with them?

## Short answer

Memory **alone** is no longer a difference. ChatGPT (saved memories + chat history), Gemini (personal context) and Claude
(account memory) all remember a person, and Replika / Nomi sell memory as their headline ("memory and a soul").

What none of them do, as far as our searches found:

1. **Memory of a family, not of one account.** One notebook shared by the parent, the child and the grandparents, the same on
   WhatsApp, Telegram and the web. The giants remember *you*; EspaLuz remembers *who is who in your house*.
2. **Memory tied to a place.** Country packs (21 countries, only "checked" lines reach the model) give the exact phrase people
   use *here*, not a textbook translation. The giants are placeless.
3. **It comes to where the family already is.** Inside WhatsApp and Telegram, follow-ups after a lesson, and a road mode that
   works with no signal for a child in the car.
4. **A teacher, not an answer machine.** Every grown-up reply is bilingual by construction; a child walks a 12-unit class path.
   ChatGPT answers; EspaLuz makes the family more independent every week.

One line: **EspaLuz is the one companion that knows your whole family, knows your country, and lives where you already talk.**

## The honest scale (production DB, 13 Nov 2026)

| What | Number |

The difference is real in the code and proven on one family. It is not yet proven on strangers. That is what the 12 Play
testers are for.

## On "nobody wants AI"

Agreed, and that is why the pitch must not be "AI" or "memory". Families buy the outcome: *settle in faster, never stuck
alone at the counter, the child keeps up at school.* Memory is the mechanism that makes it work; it belongs in "How EspaLuz is
built", not in the headline.
