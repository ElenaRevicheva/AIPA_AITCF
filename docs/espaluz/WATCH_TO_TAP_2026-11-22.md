# EspaLuz: people watch the films and do not tap (22 Nov 2026)

## The measured fact (GA4 engaged sessions + bot DB + truth layer, checked 22 Nov)
- Film 02 v18 EN: 112 YouTube views in ~16 h, IG Reel + FB Page live. **0 people came to the app or the bots from it.**
- All films, last 7 days: **8 real visitors** to the app (stayed and looked); 51 link-preview robots; 20 of our own test opens.
  The most real visitors (5) still come from film 01's old Facebook post.
- New people by ANY door since the v18 post (coded or typed by hand): **0**. The app itself works when people do arrive
  (30 days: 20 classroom opens, 4 chats started, 3 sign-ups).
- So the leak is **watch -> tap**, before the landing page. Measurement now separates people from robots (report fixed 22 Nov).

## Why (plain words)
1. **Shorts and Reels have no tappable link.** On a phone the caption URL is dead text; the path is feed -> profile -> bio
   link -> page: three taps out of a feed built for swiping. Most viewers never make that trip.
2. **The ask costs effort.** "Write to @espaluz on WhatsApp" = leave the app, open WhatsApp, search a number, think of
   what to write. No instant reward is promised for doing it.
3. **The viewers are probably not the families.** 112 views on a new Short are the platform's random test audience, not
   settling families in Latin America. A view is not a prospect.
4. Facebook is the only channel with tappable links, and it still brings the most real visitors.
