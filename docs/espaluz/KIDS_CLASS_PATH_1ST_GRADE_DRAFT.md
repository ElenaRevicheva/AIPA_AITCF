## What stays, what is added (no new system)
- **Conversation mode (today):** she talks, Pere & Titi follow HER ideas, her own sentences come back (spaced
  repetition), the parent report. Unchanged.
- **Class mode (new):** she (or a parent) says **"clase"** (web button "Clase", WhatsApp `clase`, Telegram `/clase`).
  The same kids brain runs a short structured class (~10 minutes, 5-6 exchanges) on the next unit of the path.
  Built on what exists: the lesson steps (`KIDS_STEPS`), the Pere/Titi dialogue, the sentence check, the memory game,
  the stars and the parent report. **No new table:** the unit she is on is worked out from her saved class turns,
  exactly like the spaced repetition.

## How ONE class runs (Pere = the classic path, Titi = the twist, Alisa = the judge)
1. **Hola + memory game** — one of her own sentences comes back; Titi gets it wrong, she corrects him.
2. **Reading corner (Pere, slow and clear)** — today's letter / syllable family / word; Titi "reads it wrong" on purpose
   ("¡ma-me-mu-mo... MIAU!") and she catches him. She reads one short line aloud (the sentence check listens).
3. **Words of the unit** — 2-3 useful sentences of the unit theme, Spanish ↔ English; Pere's calm version, Titi's
   funny version, "¿Quién tiene razón?".
4. **Numbers corner** — counting / adding / subtracting with real things (mangos, toucans, her toys), at the unit's level.
5. **Calm + goodbye (Pere)** — three breaths, one feelings sentence, a star, a mission for real life.
Her own idea still wins: if she brings a topic, the class bends to it (the existing rule).

## The path (12 units, ~2 classes each; order to confirm)
| Unit | Theme (both languages) | Spanish reading (lectoescritura) | English reading | Numbers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yo y mi familia / Me and my family | vowels a e i o u | letters & sounds a, m, s, t | count to 10 |
| 2 | Mi escuela / My school | m, p + vowels (ma-me-mi-mo-mu) | short a words (cat, map) | numbers to 20, before/after |
| 3 | Mi cuerpo y mis sentimientos / Body & feelings | s, l (sa-se, la-le) | sight words I, the, is, my | add within 10 |
| 4 | Animales de Panamá / Panama's animals | t, n | short i words | subtract within 10 |
| 5 | La comida / Food (mango, piña) | d, r (soft) | sight words can, see, like | count by 2s, 5s, 10s |
| 6 | Mi casa / My home | c/qu, g (soft) | short o words | numbers to 50 |
| 7 | El tiempo y el clima / Weather | f, b/v | short u words | add within 20 |
| 8 | La hora / Telling time | ll, ñ, ch | sight words go, we, you | o'clock and half past |
| 9 | En la tienda / At the shop | syllables → whole words | short e words | coins, numbers to 100 |
| 10 | Mi ciudad, Panamá / My city | short sentences (3-5 words) | blending CVC words | subtract within 20 |
| 11 | Las fiestas de Panamá / Holidays | reading a mini-story | reading a 2-line story | tens and ones |
| 12 | Mis amigos / My friends | writing her own sentence (said aloud) | her own sentence in English | review game |

Sources (checked 1 Nov 2026): MEDUCA "Derechos Fundamentales de Aprendizaje — Español" (2017) lists first-grade areas
(oral communication, reading, writing); a Panama curriculum study notes Panama starts addition and subtraction in first
grade (Programa de Matemática 1°-3°, 2014). US first-grade standards (print concepts, phonological awareness, phonics
and word recognition, high-frequency words, fluency) shape the English column.
