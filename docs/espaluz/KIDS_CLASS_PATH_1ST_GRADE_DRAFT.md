## What the best international bilingual schools use for ages 6-7 (Elena: "not only Panama - the best of the best")
EspaLuz's path is built on the frameworks international bilingual schools teach to, so a family moving between
Panama, Spain, the US or an IB school finds the same skills:
- **Cambridge Primary English, Stage 1 (ages ~5-6, framework 0058):** five strands - phonics, spelling & vocabulary;
  grammar & punctuation; reading; writing; speaking & listening. Stage 1 reading includes rhyme and patterned language
  and a story's beginning, middle and end.
- **Cambridge Primary Mathematics, Stage 1 (framework 0096):** numbers to 20 (read, write, count objects), addition by
  counting on and subtraction by counting back / "take away", time and measurement, money.
- **IB Primary Years Programme (PYP) language scope & sequence:** oral, visual and written language as separate
  strands - a child can be at one phase in speaking and another in writing, so EspaLuz tracks them separately.
- **Cambridge English Pre A1 Starters (CEFR Pre-A1, ages ~6-8):** the international benchmark for a young child's
  English: about 500 words in everyday topics (family, school, body, animals, food, home, colours, numbers to 20).
- **Spanish literacy (MEDUCA first grade, and the same in Spain and Latin America):** oral communication, reading,
  writing; learning to read by syllables (ma-me-mi-mo-mu); Panama starts addition and subtraction in first grade.
**What makes EspaLuz different:** the schools teach one language per lesson; EspaLuz builds both at once, from the
child's own words (her sentences come back by spaced repetition), with two characters who disagree so she judges.

## How ONE class runs (Pere = the classic path, Titi = the twist, Alisa = the judge)
1. **Hola + memory game** — one of her own sentences comes back; Titi gets it wrong, she corrects him.
2. **Reading corner (Pere, slow and clear)** — today's letter / syllable family / word; Titi "reads it wrong" on purpose
   ("¡ma-me-mu-mo... MIAU!") and she catches him. She reads one short line aloud (the sentence check listens).
3. **Words of the unit** — 2-3 useful sentences of the unit theme, Spanish ↔ English; Pere's calm version, Titi's
   funny version, "¿Quién tiene razón?".
4. **Numbers corner** — counting / adding / subtracting with real things (mangos, toucans, her toys), at the unit's level.
5. **Calm + goodbye (Pere)** — three breaths, one feelings sentence, a star, a mission for real life.
Her own idea still wins: if she brings a topic, the class bends to it (the existing rule).

## The path (12 units, ~2 classes each; order to confirm)
| Unit | Theme (both languages; Pre A1 Starters topics) | Spanish reading (by syllables) | English (Cambridge Stage 1 phonics / words) | Numbers (Cambridge Stage 1) | Story & talk (PYP / Cambridge) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yo y mi familia / Me and my family | vowels a e i o u | sounds s a t p; I, my | count to 10 | say who is in her family |
| 2 | Mi escuela / My school | m, p + vowels (ma-me-mi-mo-mu) | i n m d; the, is | numbers to 20: read, write, before/after | retell: beginning, middle, end |
| 3 | Mi cuerpo y mis sentimientos / Body & feelings | s, l (sa-se, la-le) | g o c k; I am, I feel | add by counting on (within 10) | name a feeling and why ("porque / because") |
| 4 | Los animales / Animals (Panama + zoo) | t, n | ck e u r; can, see | take away / count back (within 10) | rhyme and patterned language |
| 5 | La comida / Food | d, r (soft) | h b f l; like, want | doubles, count by 2s | ask and answer "do you like...?" |
| 6 | Mi casa / My home | c/qu, g (soft) | short-vowel words (cat, pig, dog) | numbers to 20: more / less | describe her room |
| 7 | Los colores y la ropa / Colours & clothes | f, b/v | blending CVC words | add within 20 | say what she is wearing |
| 8 | La hora y mi día / Time and my day | ll, ñ, ch | sh, ch, th | o'clock and half past | sequence her day: first, then, last |
| 9 | En la tienda / At the shop | syllables → whole words | sight words go, we, you, here | money: coins, simple shopping | role-play buying a mango |
| 10 | Mi ciudad / My town | short sentences (3-5 words) | reading a 2-line sentence | subtract within 20 | give simple directions |
| 11 | Fiestas y cuentos / Holidays & stories | reading a mini-story | reading a mini-story | patterns and tens (10, 20...) | change a story's ending |
| 12 | Mis amigos / My friends | writing her own sentence (said aloud) | her own sentence in English | review game | tell a short story of her own |
Stretch beyond Stage 1 for readers who are ready: numbers to 100 (MEDUCA / US first grade).
