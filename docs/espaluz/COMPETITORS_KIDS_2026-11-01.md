That combination is EspaLuz's honest edge. EspaLuz Family is $11/month, below Buddy/Lingokids, for two languages.

## What competitors do that EspaLuz can match surgically (no new systems)
1. **Spaced repetition of the child's OWN sentences** (Buddy: "active retrieval, spaced repetition"). EspaLuz already stores
   every sentence she said (`learning_sessions`) and brings one back at the hello (kids memory card). Surgical: pick the
   sentence due by a 1-3-7-day rhythm instead of "newest first".
2. **Age bands** (Buddy: 3–5 / 5–6 / 6–7 programs). Already on the Premium roadmap; the kids brain takes the age as text.
3. **Pronunciation feedback** (Studycat VoicePlay). EspaLuz has the sentence check ("¡Casi! Se dice…") — already live.

## What competitors have that EspaLuz should NOT copy
Thousands of pre-made activities, app-store apps, live human teachers: each is a rebuild, and none is the edge.

## The real gap is not features — it is families finding EspaLuz
Buddy grew by app stores and ads. EspaLuz's wedge is "no app to install — your child talks to Pere & Titi in WhatsApp".
Today the site's WhatsApp button does not start the kids lesson, and no family outside Elena's own uses the product.
