# EspaLuz classroom — what to adapt from the best programs for ages 6–8 (4 Nov 2026)

Elena: "the last day of stuffing our software with the best from the West" — bilingual international schools,
Cambridge, Eton, Latin American schools; ages 6–8; free; nothing for toddlers.

**Rule for every item:** we adapt the *teaching method* (free to use) and write our *own* content. We never copy a
publisher's book, worksheet or PDF. Exceptions where the material itself is openly licensed are marked.

## Already in the classroom (so not repeated here)
12-unit first-grade path (Cambridge Stage 1 / Pre A1 Starters / PYP / MEDUCA), letter/syllable/number/phrase tracing,
🔷 shape tracing, 47 coloring pages incl. mazes and "¿qué va primero?" sequences, 🎵 rhythm game + listening corner,
Titi's brain breaks and Pere's breathing, the Mamá–child bridge, local Panama facts.

## Honest note on Eton
Eton College takes boys **13–18** (EtonX courses are for teenagers too) — nothing there for a 6-year-old. The free
British material that *does* fit 6–8 is the England **Key Stage 1** curriculum and DfE phonics (see #5).

## The candidates, ranked for Alisa (fit × impact × cost)

| # | Feature | Comes from | What it builds (6–8) | Free status | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"¿Qué ves? ¿Qué piensas? ¿Qué te preguntas?"** — after she paints or sends a photo, Pere and Titi ask these three in order | Harvard Project Zero thinking routine *See–Think–Wonder*; also IB PYP inquiry | observing, explaining, asking questions (IB "thinkers", Cambridge GP research 1Rs.01 "ask basic questions") | The routine is free to use **with credit** ("developed by Project Zero, Harvard GSE"); their PDFs are CC BY-NC-ND → write our own words, don't copy the handout | small (kids brain prompt) |
| 2 | **🐒 Programa a Titi** — arrow cards (⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️) make Titi walk a grid to the banana; later "find the error" (debugging) | Cambridge Primary Computing Stage 1 ("algorithm", "sequence", "instructions", "error"); England KS1 computing; unplugged robot games | algorithms, sequencing, debugging — and it connects to the step-by-step pages | method free; our own code and art | medium (new pad mode) |
| 3 | **Números con las manos** — ten-frame, number bonds to 10 and 20, simple bar model with fruit; telling time to the half hour; Panama coins (balboa/dólar) | Singapore Math (concrete → pictorial → abstract); Cambridge Primary Maths Stage 1 | number sense beyond tracing digits | method free | medium |
| 4 | **Proyecto de la semana con mamá** — one real question a week ("¿De dónde viene el agua de nuestra casa?", "¿Qué comen las familias en Panamá?"); she asks a family member, draws the answer, tells Pere and Titi; a "mapa de mi barrio" and "historia de mi familia" | Cambridge Primary **Global Perspectives** Stage 1 (topics: family & community, health, water, food & farming, planet; skills: research, collaboration, reflection); **Escuela Nueva** (Colombia: family histories, community maps, self-paced guides); IB PYP units of inquiry | research and reflection; and **the family bridge for expat families** — the project is done *together* | methods free | small–medium (prompt + one page) |
| 5 | **English phonics games** — oral blending and segmenting ("c–a–t → cat"), sound-spotting, rhymes; next to the Spanish syllable work she already does | DfE *Letters and Sounds* (England, 2007) — Phase One is published under the **Open Government Licence** (DERA archive); England KS1 phonics | reading English, not just speaking it | Phase One OGL = reusable with attribution (confirm the licence of any other phase before reuse) | medium |
| 6 | **¿Cómo me siento? check-in** — at the start of class she picks a feeling from a richer bilingual set (orgullosa → proud, frustrada → frustrated, nerviosa → nervous) and Pere offers one strategy | CASEL SEL competencies (self-awareness, self-management); IB "balanced"; Cambridge Wellbeing | emotional vocabulary in two languages — important in relocation | framework free; avoid trademarked tools (e.g. "Mood Meter") | small |
| 7 | **Cualidad de la semana** — one trait per week (curiosa/curious, valiente/brave, amable/caring…), the heroes notice it in what she does | IB Learner Profile (10 attributes) | character language | idea free; use our own words, don't brand it "IB" | small |
| 8 | **Misión de la vida real** — a real home task once a day (set the table, water the plant, pack the backpack), mamá confirms → ⭐ | Montessori "practical life"; links to the routine sequences | independence, order — not toddler chores, real ones | free | small |
| 9 | **Muestra y cuenta (Show and tell)** — she shows a real object to the camera and speaks 3 sentences with stems ("Esto es… / Me gusta porque… / Lo uso para…") | Oracy teaching in UK and international schools | structured speaking, both languages | method free | small |

## Recommended for today (biggest value, lowest risk, all additive)
1. **#1 See–Think–Wonder** on her paintings and photos (kids brain only — the screen does not change).
2. **#4 Proyecto de la semana con mamá** (the family bridge + Global Perspectives + Escuela Nueva in one).
