# EspaLuz classroom — music options (4 Nov 2026)

Trigger: Alisa liked the songs in Buddy.ai and asked for music in the Pere & Titi classroom. Constraint: no copyright risk.

## What Buddy.ai actually shows (checked 4 Nov 2026)
- Official page (buddy.ai/en/english-for-kids): ages 3–7; lessons = warm-up talk, presentation with "flashcards,
  animations, video" and **sounds**, voice practice, games, cartoons. **No mention of songs** on the official page.
- Third-party listings (weak sources: app catalogs) say lessons include "fun and engaging songs" and that music is
  one of the covered subjects. So: songs exist in the app per catalogs, not confirmed by the official page.
- Known for songs in this category: Lingokids (large library of shows and songs) — a competitor, not Buddy.

## Copyright-safe sources we already have (Atuona machinery, cto-aipa)
- **Pixabay music** — the Atuona film compiler's royalty-free library started with a Pixabay track (June 2026).
  Pixabay Content License: free for commercial use in apps, no attribution required; cannot be resold as standalone
  music. Instrumental only = background beds, not learning songs.
- **ElevenLabs Music** — Atuona film #9's own track was made with it (`elevenlabs/music` in cto-aipa src). Generates
  original songs **with vocals from our own lyrics**. Commercial use depends on the ElevenLabs plan — verify the plan
  before shipping anything public.
- **Traditional / public-domain melodies** ("Los pollitos dicen", "Cabeza, hombros, rodillas y pies", "Twinkle
  Twinkle", "Old MacDonald"): the melody and words are free, but someone else's *recording* is not — we would need our
  own recording (e.g. generated).

## Recommendation (not built — waiting for Elena's go)
1. **Learning songs (the real value):** 12 short original bilingual songs, one per classroom unit (colours, numbers,
   family, routines "primero / después / al final", shapes, Panama animals), lyrics written by us from the unit's own
   words, sung by a generated voice. A 🎵 button in the classroom plays the unit's song; Titi's brain break can use it.
2. **Background bed:** 3–4 Pixabay instrumental tracks, low volume, only while painting or tracing, with a mute switch.
3. Never: popular kids' songs or cartoon songs (Baby Shark, Disney…) — recordings and lyrics are protected.
