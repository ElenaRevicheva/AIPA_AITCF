## What schools do (sources)
- Cambridge Early Years: EY1 ages 3-4, EY2 4-5, EY3 5-6; for ages 5-6 schools use EY3 OR Primary Stage 1; phonics runs
  through Cambridge Phonics & Handwriting Steps. https://learning.cambridgeinternational.org/classroom/mod/book/tool/print/index.php?id=154405 ,
  https://WWW.CAMBRIDGEINTERNATIONAL.ORG/Images/710812-cambridge-early-years-a-guide-for-parents.pdf
- Panama MEDUCA: Pre-jardín at 4, Jardín (kinder) at 5 (Decreto Ejecutivo 1134 of 2010, cited in MEDUCA guidelines 2024);
  preschool books centre on reading as communication. https://www.meduca.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Lineamientos-2024_editado.pdf
- Bilingual IB PYP example: Kindergarten = 90% Spanish / 10% English, initial reading taught in Spanish; grades 2-5 50/50.
  https://corralesis.org/?p=341
- Stage numbering varies by source (one lists Cambridge Stage 2 English for ages 6-7) - check the official framework
  before naming stages on a public page. https://www.twinkl.com/resources/cambridge-primary-curriculum/english-cambridge-primary-curriculum-international-schools/stage-2-english-cambridge-primary-curriculum-international-schools/2

## Plan: one classroom, three levels, chosen by the child's age in the family list
Same 12 themes for every level (Me and my family, My school, ... My friends) - schools spiral themes; only the goals and
the sentence length change. One table, three goal columns; no new screens.
| Level | Age | Spanish | English | Numbers | Talk rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinder (Jardín, EY3) | 5 | vowels, letter sounds, rhymes, tracing strokes, own name | words + sounds, songs, colours | count and recognise to 10, shapes | 3-6 words per sentence, more Spanish, more movement and songs, one idea per line |
| 1st grade (Stage 1) | 6-7 | TODAY'S 12 units (syllables -> blends -> mini-stories) | Starters words and sounds | to 20 / 100 as today | as today (6-12 words) |
| 2nd grade (Stage 2, Starters) | 7-8 | read short stories aloud, write her own 2-3 sentences, accents, capital letters | read and answer simple questions, short sentences | to 100 / 1000, add and take away, time, money | 8-15 words, asks "why", retells a story |
- Level comes from the child's age (family list); the parent can move it up or down in one tap (a child of 5 who reads
  already). Parent report names the level.
- Then, and only then, the site says "ages 5-8"; `pr.p4` says what is true (e.g. "Brothers and sisters - each child at
  their own level, ages 5-8, in one family plan").
- Russian heritage class (Mishka & Lisa) keeps its own ages; not touched here.
