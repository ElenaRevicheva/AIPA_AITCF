## What they all agree on
- TIME: 45-75 min a WEEK. Weekday steps 5-10 min; one longer slot at the weekend; at least one day off.
- CHANNEL: WhatsApp/Telegram first, voice notes welcome; the web for the kids' class and a rehearsal.
- REMINDERS: 2-3 MORNINGS a week (7:45-9:00), one concrete line with a task; NEVER after 7 pm, never guilt or streak
  pressure, never daily pings. Their own wording: "Morning Jennifer! 2 words for the school gate today. 2 mins to listen?"
- PROGRESS: real-life moments, not XP - "handled the school note without Google Translate", "ordered at the fonda"; a split
  per person/track (me: Spanish or English; my child: class).
- CHILD: the class card + a tiny summary on the parent's phone of what the child said; Marina: Мишка & Лиса ON the class card.
- ALREADY WORKS for them: Phrases you've collected (+ 🔊), the child's class card with her own sentences, the first-year
  calendar (settlers), face-to-face prep, one memory across WhatsApp/Telegram/web.
