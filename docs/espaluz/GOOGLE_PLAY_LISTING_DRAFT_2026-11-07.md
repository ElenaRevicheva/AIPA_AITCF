## Full description — English (en-US)
Moving to Latin America with your family, or working from there as a digital nomad? EspaLuz is your bilingual companion for the long road: she helps you live there in Spanish, one real moment at a time.

EspaLuz is an AI companion. You write or send a voice note, in English or Spanish, and she answers in both languages,
with the Spanish phrase you need right now: at school, at the doctor, at the bank, at the market.

WHAT YOU GET
• A warm chat in Spanish and English, with voice replies.
• She remembers your family: names, your child's school, what you asked last week, so you do not start from zero.
• Pere and Titi's classroom for children: a playful Spanish class with two animated friends, that continues where your
  child left off.
• A short report for the parent after the class.
• The same EspaLuz on WhatsApp and Telegram, if you prefer to talk there.

HOW IT WORKS
Sign up free for 14 days. Then one monthly subscription through Google Play. Cancel any time in Google Play.

GOOD TO KNOW
• EspaLuz is an AI, not a person. For emergencies, always contact local emergency services.
• The kids classroom opens only after a grown-up signs in.
• No ads. We do not sell your data or train AI models on your conversations. Privacy: https://app.espaluz.lat/privacy-policy
