Line they illustrate: **"EspaLuz: live your new country in Spanish."** Each scene shows one real moment the product
helps with today (doctor + voice interpreter, public office, school note photo, local words, kids learning).

## How to use (one time read)
1. **Image first, then video.** Make the still in Imagine, pick the best one, then press *Make video* on it
   (image-to-video keeps the faces and the light; text-to-video drifts).
2. **Format:** add `vertical 9:16` for Reels / TikTok / Shorts / Stories; `16:9` for YouTube and the page.
3. **No words on screen.** AI letters come out garbled. Captions and the EspaLuz button are added after.
4. **No real logos, flags on uniforms, or real hospital/office names.** Generic, believable places.
5. **Pere and Titi:** the engine cannot draw *our* sloth and monkey from words. For them, upload our own picture
   (`aideazz.xyz/espaluz-classroom.jpg`) and animate it (prompt 7).
6. Every post that uses these says: *"People in this video are AI-generated."* (honest, and Meta asks for it).
