**Q: Which AI video tools have you used in production?**
For Crimson Escape I tested the same keyframe and motion on six engines: Wan 2.7, Grok Imagine 1.5, HappyHorse 1.0,
Veo 3.1 Fast, Kling 3.0 Omni and Seedance 2.5. I cast Wan for emotional close-ups and Grok for wide shots. Keyframes
come from Flux 2 Max. My production bot also has Sora 2 Pro, PixVerse, Hailuo and others wired in.
*If they push — Runway, Pika?* Not used. Same method applies: one shot, every engine, pick by result and price.

**Q: How do you keep characters consistent across shots?**
Each lead is fixed by one reference portrait, and every keyframe is generated with that portrait as input, so the same
two people carry the whole film. When a healed scar started reading as a fresh wound on close-ups, I made a healed
version of the reference, added the wound terms to the negative prompt and re-rendered those four shots.

**Q: How do you choose a model for a shot?**
A bake-off, not habit: same keyframe, same five-second motion, six engines, side by side. Price per second ranged from
$0.08 to $0.23, and one engine refused the shot outright. Then I pick per shot type.

**Q: Premiere Pro / After Effects?** *(honest gap)*
No. I finish in my own scripted pipeline: cuts, dissolves, hard-cut glitch inserts, motion-interpolated retiming (I
slowed one character by 20% so her breathing felt real), letterbox crops, and loudness mastered to −15.7 LUFS. The
decisions are the same ones an editor makes; I would learn your NLE quickly.

**Q: Four years of professional video?** *(honest gap)*
AI video since 2025: eight finished, published films. Before that, seven years as Deputy CEO delivering against a
brief and a budget, which is most of what "own the production end to end" means.

**Q: ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion, LoRAs?** *(honest gap)*
Not yet — my stack is hosted models through their APIs. The habit that transfers is evaluation: I test before I commit.

**Q: Budget and cost control?**
The pipeline prices every job before it runs and stops at a hard cap. Crimson Escape cost about $18 in generation.
Once the budget guard could never fire because it read a price field the vendor never sends; I found it, fixed it and
published the postmortem.

**Q: Explicit / Companion Mode content?**
My films work in an adult, sensual register, and I am comfortable with explicit content. The line I hold: never
sexualise a real person's likeness, and never strip a safety filter off a real person's photo.
