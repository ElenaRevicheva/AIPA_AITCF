## 1. One piece of work you shipped and now hate, with the reason (≤200 words) — DRAFT, 136 words

The first cut of Crimson Escape, my eighth film. I published it and replaced it the same afternoon.

Two things. Shot eight: the engine rewrote my prompt on its own and added scratches to her face. Nobody asked for
them. It is exactly the kind of tell that says a machine decided, not a director. I re-rendered it with the model's
prompt rewriting switched off.

And two shots of a man loading a jeep and driving her across the island. Beautiful, and useless: they explained a
journey the poem had already made. I cut them. The film now goes from the airstrip straight to her bed, and it is
17 seconds shorter and much less polite.

What I hate is not the shots. It is that I shipped before I watched it as a stranger would.
