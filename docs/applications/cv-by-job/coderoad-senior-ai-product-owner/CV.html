Own the AI Product Backlog & Strategy
  • AI Visibility Audit API — idea to launch to measured use. A public product, launched July 2026: anyone pastes a website address and, with no login, gets a score for how well ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude can find and cite it. I designed the rubric, 34 checks in 4 weighted categories, and an output a non-technical buyer can act on: a grade, and the fix and the reason for every failed check. In the server logs for 1–10 October alone: 39 audits of 18 websites — 18 of them run by people on the public page and 21 by my own outreach agents before a new prospect is contacted.
  • Prioritized from outcome data. In my job-search pipeline, outcome labels showed one role category made up 7 of the 12 postings I rejected but only 2 of the 12 I rated positive. I cut it from targeting within a day and re-pointed the searches to the categories that fit.
  • Said no on evidence. Two job boards looked equally good on their websites, but one had no posting newer than 33 days and 91% past their own expiry. I rejected it, connected the other, and wrote down why.
  • Acceptance criteria: what the model decides and what code decides. The spec says what the model decides and what code decides: hard facts in a posting, such as a country restriction or a pay figure, are checked in code; judgment calls go to the model. When one of my override rules wrongly let three postings through because it saw "LATAM" and ignored "U.S. only", I changed it to ask the same location checker the filters use.
Establish AI Quality & Evaluation Frameworks
  • My acceptance criteria are tests: a change is accepted only when the tests and the production logs prove it works. 600+ automated tests on the job-discovery pipeline (unit, integration, golden-set), running in about a minute.
  • Golden evaluation set — accuracy beats sophistication. An upgrade to my AI screening step (retrieval, RAG) scored lower on a test set built from my own past decisions, so it stayed switched off: a change that measures worse does not ship, however sophisticated.
  • I fix the measurement itself: my screening replay was grading against blank postings, so its score measured the blank. Each decision is now stored with its real posting and replayed before a change ships.
  • Hallucination guardrail. When a provider balance ran out, my daily publisher fell to a cheaper fallback model and for ten weeks described a database the system has never used, while every run reported clean. I stopped it at the source and rebuilt it to write only from facts measured on the running system: an article with a number absent from that bundle, or naming infrastructure that is not installed, is rejected and regenerated; if the model still cannot stay inside that bundle, an evidence-only article is composed from the licensed numbers, and a day with too few verified facts gets no article.
Balance rapid innovation with operational stability
