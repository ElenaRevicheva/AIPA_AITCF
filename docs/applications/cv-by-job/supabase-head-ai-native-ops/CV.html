How I Operate

Standards as executable systems. Recurring rules are encoded into workflows, deterministic gates, evaluations and reports — not left to memory.

Human accountability by design. AI handles repeatable execution; consequential actions keep explicit approval and a named owner.

Evidence over automation theatre. A workflow reporting “success” is not proof the business outcome happened; I verify in the receiving system.

Failure becomes infrastructure. Silent failures, bad assumptions and regressions become named postmortems, controls and evaluation cases.
