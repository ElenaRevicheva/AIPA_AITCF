## Does it fit Toptal style?

**No.** It is a strong founder / GEO-operator CV. Toptal is a staffing desk.

What they skim for, and what this PDF does:

1. **One specialty.** You list AI Automation Architect, Applied AI Engineer, Growth Operator, Forward-Deployed, Stream Lead, Internal Tools, AI Ops, GEO-AEO Lead. A matcher cannot put that in one search bucket. Pick **Applied AI / automation engineer** — production LLM systems for CRM, approved outreach, and AI-search visibility.
2. **Client-shaped outcomes, not a lab tour.** “Twelve systems I run for myself” is true and impressive to a founder. To Toptal it is unpaid self-employment unless EspaLuz and the public API carry the page.
3. **Two-audience tables** (“if you run a business / if you run engineering”). Clever for `aideazz.xyz`. Wrong for a screener. They want: problem → what you shipped → measurable result.
4. **“Open to Full-time · Contract · Remote” + Target roles.** Job-seeker footer. Toptal wants: contract, hours/week, timezone, overlap with US/EU. Example: *Contract · 20–30h/week · Panama UTC-5 · overlap 09:00–13:00 ET.*
5. **English (Advanced).** Your written English is fluent. “Advanced” is the box that gets non-native applicants filtered. On Toptal say **Professional working / fluent** if you will do the video screen in English (they score clarity, not accent).
6. **Page 3 is dilution.** In-progress Anthropic cert, Polkadot, How-To-DAO, ICO, Fundery — none of that is the specialty you are selling. OmniBazaar with **zero bullets** looks like a hole. One outcome line or drop it.
7. **Deputy CEO, JSC “E-GOV OPERATOR” (Russia).** Keep it — honesty, and it explains board-level conversations. Do not let it close the resume. One line, regulated digital transformation, then stop. Some US enterprise clients will bounce; hiding it is worse.
8. **`$0/month` infrastructure.** Keep only if paired with load (420+ audits, 1,900 deals). Alone it reads as hobby scale.
9. **Three pages.** Stage 1 is a skim. Two pages. Portfolio URL in the header is the third page.

Toptal’s own screen (language + personality, then a live exercise, then a timed project) rewards people who can say **what the client needed, what you shipped, what you told them when scope moved.** Practice that out loud on three stories. The PDF cannot do that work.
