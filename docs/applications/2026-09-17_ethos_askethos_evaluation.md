# Ethos (agent.askethos.com) — is it AI training? Worth it? (17 Sep 2026)

**Verdict: YES, apply — it costs one 20–30 minute voice call.** It is *partly* AI training.
Ethos is an AI-run **expert network**, not a pure AI-training marketplace like Surge or Mercor.

## What it is
- London startup founded 2024 by James Lo (ex-McKinsey, SoftBank) and Daniel Mankowitz (ex-DeepMind:
  Gemini, AlphaDev). Series A **$22.75M led by a16z** (TechCrunch, 6 May 2026). Takes 30%+ per project.
- Clients (unnamed): hedge funds, private equity, **"leading foundational AI labs"**, enterprise consulting.
- No CV form. You do a **voice interview with an AI agent**; the transcript plus your public work
  (repos, articles, talks) becomes a searchable profile. Clients' searches match you to:
  1. **AI training / evaluation** for AI labs (stress-testing model output in your domain)
  2. **Paid advisory calls** (30–60 min)
  3. Market research / surveys
  4. **Fractional and full-time roles**
- Pay reported by third-party trackers: **$35–$225/hr, median ~$80/hr** (87 listings). Independent
  contractor, **W-8BEN** for non-US, paid **Net-30** after the project. "Open globally"; only
  licensed roles (US tax, US legal) are country-locked.

## Risks, honestly
- **No guaranteed work.** ~35,000 people join per week (TechCrunch). Some reviewers interviewed and
  never got matched, and felt the long AI interview was free data collection. Ethos's written policy
  says call data is not used to train its models and can be deleted. Not independently verifiable.
- Trustpilot 4.2/5 (199 reviews, 71% five-star, 14% one-star). Praise: on-time payment once matched.
- Net-30: first money arrives about a month after the first project, not this week.

## Why it fits Elena
- Four doors from one call: AI-lab evaluation (her chosen income path — sell judgment, not code),
  paid advisory calls, **fractional** AI roles, full-time roles. Pre-aggregated demand, which is the
  missing channel (see `project_root_cause_no_demand_channel`).
- **Matching rewards specificity.** "I'm in AI" gets zero matches. Name the niches only she combines:
  1. **Legal + regulatory + e-government** — seven years Deputy CEO & Chief Legal Officer, board-level
     digital transformation in a regulated environment (rare in AI evaluation pools).
  2. **AI agents and automation in production** — twelve live systems solo; five-provider LLM failover
     ordered by use case; an LLM judge whose criteria drifted across seven layers and the registry
     + contract tests that fixed it.
  3. **GEO / AEO** — a live public AI-visibility audit API.
  4. **Evaluating LLM output** — silent failures where the tool reported success and it wasn't.
- State real availability (hours/week) — overstating it lowers future placement priority.
