## How the list was made (reproducible)
- Google Places Text Search (New), the same request `src/prospect-places.ts` makes, `regionCode=PA`, 5 queries, 100 results. The key works (HTTP 200).
- Of 100 results, **12 had no website on their Google profile**: yacht charter Panama 2/20, dental clinic Panama City 7/20,
  dental implants Panama 3/20, relocation services Panama 0/17, boat charter Pearl Islands 0/3.
- **"No website on Google" ≠ "no website".** Panama Yacht Adventures had no Google link but has a site (and a `[CLIENT-ATLAS]` deal since
  21 Sep). So each candidate was web-searched. Dropped: Flamenco Marina (a marina, no phone), Biohorizons Panama (implant brand, not a clinic).
- **Result: 9 dental clinics. A web search turned up no website for any of them; directory listings (mostly WhatClinic) were all it found.**

## Findings worth keeping
- In Panama, the no-website high-ticket segment is **dental**, not yachts or relocation (every relocation firm found has a site).
- Most are **small** (0–27 reviews). Only TLDC has a big reputation (4.9★, 401 reviews).
- The pitch is concrete: *the only pages about you are directories like WhatClinic, which send the same patients to your competitors.*
