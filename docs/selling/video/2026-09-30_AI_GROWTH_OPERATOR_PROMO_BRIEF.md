## 1. What the two decks actually say

**The offer (AI Growth Operator deck).** An *installed* AI-native growth system for **high-ticket service businesses**:
"Be discoverable. Capture demand. Qualify it. Follow up. Keep the CRM alive."
- **Problem:** high-value demand is fragmented across AI search, websites, WhatsApp, outreach and CRM. Prospects research
  through AI assistants first; inquiries arrive by form/email/WhatsApp but qualification and follow-up are manual; CRM goes
  stale and "good opportunities quietly age out". *"Buying another dashboard does not solve the operating gap."*
- **Opportunity:** connect discovery → conversation → CRM → follow-up → measurement **as one operating loop**.
- **Modules (one operator):** AI visibility · research · qualification · outreach · follow-up · CRM · reporting · memory.
- **Proof it is real ("an operating system, not a demo"):** HubSpot provenance per agent; Claude tool-use research loop
  (SERP → Web Unlocker → browser); Telegram control plane; human approval before sends with CRM write-back;
  NEW / ACTIVE / AGING freshness.
- **ICP = a buying pattern, not an industry:** sale > ~$2,000 · leads online, closed by a person · WhatsApp/phone in the
  path · international/English audience · margins for a managed system · no in-house AI team.
  **Strong early fits: medical tourism · dental implants / cosmetic dentistry · immigration & relocation · luxury tourism / charters.**
- **Success = commercial fruit, not "the automation ran":** Discovery → Motion → Conversation → **Cash**.
- **Doctrine (the single best line for the video):** *"Buyers do not want 'AI.' They want more patients, bookings,
  consultations and signed contracts."*
- **CTA:** Install an AI Growth Operator · aideazz.xyz/portfolio · AI visibility audit: **aideazz.xyz/api**.

**The operator (Professional Outlook deck).** "I design the system, direct AI-assisted implementation, ship it, operate
it and own the outcome." *"I do not sell unaided typing speed."* Three value zones — AI Operations · AI Product &
Transformation · **Creative AI Systems** — one pattern: *"I design intelligent systems and the experience around those systems."*

**Why the two belong in one video.** The offer deck says *what gets installed*; the outlook deck says *who owns it*.
The Atuona film studio is itself the proof of the third zone — the promo can be **made by the same kind of system it
sells**, and say so.

## 3. Creative concept (proposal) — "She asked ChatGPT first"

**Format:** a 75–90 s YouTube film (16:9) + a 30 s vertical Short cut from the same shots.
**Story unit:** one high-ticket buyer, one business, one lost-then-won inquiry. Example vertical: **dental implants /
medical tourism in Panama** (ICP fit #1–2, and Panama is home ground). Alternatives: relocation, luxury charter.

| Time | Beat | Picture | Words (draft, plain) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | **Hook** | A woman abroad, late evening, types to an AI assistant: *"best dental implant clinic in Panama"* | "Before your next patient calls you… she asks an AI." |
| 5–20 s | **The leak** | The answer names other clinics. Meanwhile at the clinic: a WhatsApp inquiry sits unanswered; a CRM card turns grey — *AGING* | "If the AI can't read you, it won't recommend you. And the ones who do write… wait." |
| 20–32 s | **The turn (REAL screen)** | aideazz.xyz/api — URL typed, score counting up, 34 checks, categories filling | "So we start with a free audit: 34 checks. What AI can see, and what it can't." |
| 32–60 s | **The loop installed** | Fast, clean montage: research → qualified WhatsApp reply drafted → **a human taps Approve** → follow-up task → deal moves stage → morning brief in Telegram | "Discovery. Conversation. Follow-up. CRM. Every step visible. Nothing sent without a human yes." |
| 60–72 s | **Doctrine** | The same woman, now at the clinic, handshake / consultation — warm, real, no stock props | "Nobody buys AI. They buy more patients, bookings and signed contracts." |
| 72–85 s | **Proof + maker** | Title card: AIdeazz AI Lab · Elena Revicheva — "I design the system, ship it, operate it and own the outcome." Small line: *This film was made by our own AI film pipeline.* | (Elena's line, ideally in her own voice) |
| 85–90 s | **CTA** | aideazz.xyz/api — free audit · $100 diagnostic | "Run your free AI visibility audit today." |

## 4. How we produce it (Atuona studio, already built)

- **Generated shots** (~14–18): Wan 2.7 for close emotional shots, Grok Imagine 1.5 for wide, Kling 3.0 Omni where a
  shot must stay literal; consistent characters via one reference portrait per character (film #8 method).
  Film #8 cost ≈ $18 in generation; budget this at **$25–40** (Replicate is prepaid — check balance before rendering).
- **Real screen captures** (the credibility core): the live API audit; a HubSpot deal with provenance prefix (Elena's
  own test data, IDs cropped); Telegram control plane — no customer data on screen.
- **Voice:** Elena's own recorded voice for the maker line (strongest), AI narrator for the rest. OpenAI TTS is **out of
  credits** (29 Sep); Gemini TTS or a Replicate TTS model work on keys already on Oracle.
- **Music:** Pixabay library, measured like film #8 (no reuse of *The Ritual*).
- **Style:** Atuona's cinema look, but **business-bright, not underground**; no model defaults (floaty drift, stock props).
  Phones are unavoidable in this story — they must look real and specific, not stock.
- **Deliverables:** 16:9 master (YouTube), 9:16 Short, thumbnail, title/description/chapters with UTM links to /api.
