## The story (the proven skeleton + the relocation film's "two businesses" merge)
Working title: *She Asked ChatGPT Before She Messaged Your Clinic*
- **Late night, Dallas or Toronto:** two sisters (about 55 and 48) plan one trip — the elder needs dental implants, the
  younger wants a cosmetic consultation. They ask ChatGPT which clinics in Panama City are best for international patients
  and reply quickly in English.
- **Timeline 1 — Friday:** the implant dentist is three hours into a surgery; the cosmetic clinic's coordinator is checking
  in another patient's family. Both reply Monday; the sisters have already booked elsewhere.
- **Rewind:** same sisters, same question; instant acknowledgement, a draft on the coordinator's phone, the clinic approves.
  The compliance spine is even stronger here: *the AI drafts — the doctor and the clinic decide.*
- **Win:** a calm, luxurious clinic welcome in the Punta Pacífica medical district (never a named hospital); payoff = the two
  sisters laughing on a Casco Viejo terrace during their recovery days.

## Guardrails (medical advertising)
No before/after, no outcomes or results promised, no prices, no procedures or surgical scenes, no patient data on screen,
no medical advice from the AI (the test inquiry asks only for a consultation time). Real screens = the proven set, with a
fresh test inquiry in the patient's voice.
