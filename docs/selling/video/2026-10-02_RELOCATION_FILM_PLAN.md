Why A won:

**Logline.** Chicago, 23:52. A 62-year-old architect has just sold her practice and is retiring to the Boquete highlands. Her 34-year-old son is buying a condo in Panama City and moving his crypto software company there. Together they ask ChatGPT one question: which real estate agencies and law firms can help them, and reply quickly in English?

- **Timeline 1.** On Friday the agent is up in the misty coffee hills showing a house to another couple. The lawyer is at the immigration office with another family. Both reply on Monday, and both read "Thanks — we've already chosen…".
- **Rewind.** Same family, same question. This time she writes to the agency through its website, and he writes to the firm through its website. Both hear back at once. On that same Friday, both owners read the drafted reply, nod and tap once.
- **The win.** The next week, mother and son step onto the cobbles of Casco Viejo and smile up at the firm's door. Later, she has her first coffee on her own terrace in Boquete as the cloud lifts off Volcán Barú.

**No visa, price, investment or crypto claim is ever shown or promised. The film sells a faster first conversation.**
