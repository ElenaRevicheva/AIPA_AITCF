SUBJECT: For Mila Askarova: poem-films from Atuona

Dear Mila,

I hope this inbox can pass my letter to you. Congratulations on the New York opening. GAZELL.iO has shown artists working with AI since 2015, and that is why I am writing.

I write under the name Kira Velerevich. My gallery is called ATUONA, after the village in the Marquesas where Gauguin is buried: the far edge a painter went to when there was nowhere further to go.

There are 99 fragments in the vault, roughly half in Russian and half in English. LITPROM published 46 of them. The rest I publish myself, in a gallery I build myself. I do not translate myself for anyone's comfort.

Then the poems started asking to move, so I began directing them as films, with AI as my camera and my own verse on the screen. Crimson Escape is the newest, 3 minutes 36 seconds long: 3 AM, a crimson room, an Olympia after Manet who stares straight back, and black volcanic sand the Pacific keeps trying to bleach.

The films: https://atuona.xyz/aifilmstudio/
The vault: https://atuona.xyz/

Would you or the GAZELL.iO team watch it? I would love to know whether there is a place for work like this in your digital programme.

With admiration, from Panama,
Elena Revicheva
writing as Kira Velerevich
