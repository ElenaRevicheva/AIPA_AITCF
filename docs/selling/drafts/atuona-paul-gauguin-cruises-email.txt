SUBJECT: A poet from Atuona, for your Marquesas voyages

Dear Paul Gauguin Cruises team,

I hope this can reach whoever curates your enrichment programme.

I write under the name Kira Velerevich. My gallery and film studio is called ATUONA, after the village on Hiva Oa where your ship calls, and where Gauguin is buried.

There are 99 fragments in the vault, roughly half in Russian and half in English. LITPROM published 46 of them. Some are set on Hiva Oa itself. One begins: "Hiva Oa doesn't accept guests. It eats them." Another is about the carved doorframes of the Maison du Jouir, which your guests see reconstructed at the Centre Culturel Paul Gauguin.

I direct the poems as films, with AI as my camera and my own verse on the screen. Eight are finished. Crimson Escape, the newest, opens with that Hiva Oa poem, Hungry Earth, and closes on the black volcanic sand of the Marquesas.

I read about Kim McDonald sailing with you to speak about Gauguin. I would love to offer the other half of that conversation: a screening and a talk on your Marquesas voyages, the evening before Atuona, about what the island does to the people who arrive, told through the poems and the films. The films are adult in mood, never explicit, and I would curate the selection for the room.

The films: https://atuona.xyz/aifilmstudio/
The vault: https://atuona.xyz/

Who would be the right person to talk to about this, and what does a guest role with you involve?
