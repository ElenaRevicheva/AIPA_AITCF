TO: diane@asvoff.com
SUBJECT: Crimson Escape: a poetry film from Atuona, for ASVOFF

Dear Diane,

I write under the name Kira Velerevich. My gallery is called ATUONA, after the village in the Marquesas where Gauguin is buried: the far edge a painter went to when there was nowhere further to go.

There are 99 fragments in the vault, roughly half in Russian and half in English. LITPROM published 46 of them. The rest I publish myself, in a gallery I build myself. I do not translate myself for anyone's comfort.

Then the poems started asking to move, so I began directing them as films, with AI as my camera and my own verse on the screen. Crimson Escape is the newest, 3 minutes 36 seconds long. It is 3 AM in a crimson room, and an Olympia after Manet stares straight back. It closes on black volcanic sand that the Pacific keeps trying to bleach, and on one line: "I will not whiten."

Fashion found its own way in. The newest poem in the vault is set backstage, among organza torn from the runway, fabric glue and failed bids. Its title is a machine's refusal, "Could not generate content." I kept it.

The films: https://atuona.xyz/aifilmstudio/
The vault: https://atuona.xyz/

I would be honoured to submit Crimson Escape to the next ASVOFF. When does the call open, and is work made with AI eligible?

With admiration, from Panama,
Elena Revicheva
