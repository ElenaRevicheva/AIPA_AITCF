TO: sasha@sashastiles.com
SUBJECT: A poet to a poet: films from Atuona

Dear Sasha,

I write under the name Kira Velerevich. My gallery is called ATUONA, after the village in the Marquesas where Gauguin is buried: the far edge a painter went to when there was nowhere further to go.

Your work with Technelegy is why I am writing. You gave your poem a second voice. I gave mine a camera.

There are 99 fragments in my vault, roughly half in Russian and half in English. LITPROM published 46 of them. The rest I publish myself, in a gallery I build myself. Then the poems started asking to move, so I began directing them as films, with AI as my camera and my own verse on the screen. Crimson Escape is the newest: 3 AM, a crimson room, an Olympia after Manet who stares straight back.

The newest poem in the vault kept a machine's refusal as its title: "Could not generate content." I think you will understand why I did not fix it.

The films: https://atuona.xyz/aifilmstudio/
The vault: https://atuona.xyz/

Would you watch one? And if you know a room where poem-films like these belong, I would be grateful for its name.

With admiration, from Panama,
Elena Revicheva
