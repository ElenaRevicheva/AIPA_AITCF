SUBJECT: For Anika Meier: poem-films from Atuona

Dear Anika,

I hope this inbox can pass my letter to you. Body Anxiety in the Age of AI is why I am writing.

I write under the name Kira Velerevich. My gallery is called ATUONA, after the village in the Marquesas where Gauguin is buried. There are 99 fragments in the vault, roughly half in Russian and half in English. LITPROM published 46 of them; the rest I publish myself, in a gallery I build myself.

Kira is also my heroine. "She doesn't speak in manifestos. She leaves traces." I direct her as films, with AI as my camera and my own verse on the screen. In every film I argue with the machine over one detail: her scar has to read as old and healed, never fresh, and never erased.

Crimson Escape is the newest, 3 minutes 36 seconds long. It is 3 AM in a crimson room, an Olympia after Manet stares straight back, and it closes on black volcanic sand with one line: "I will not whiten."

The films: https://atuona.xyz/aifilmstudio/
The vault: https://atuona.xyz/

Would you watch it? If work like this ever fits a show or a collection you are curating, I would love to be considered.

With admiration, from Panama,
Elena Revicheva
