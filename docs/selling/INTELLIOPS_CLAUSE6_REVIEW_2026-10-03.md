## Is Clause 6 good or bad?

**Mostly good, and honest.** Since 25 Aug the blocker was "IntelliOps Automation is a brand — I cannot
invoice a brand." Clause 6 answers it: the debtor is a named human being, personally responsible for
the whole debt. An individual trading under a name is a normal, valid counterparty. Noida vs New Delhi
is the same country and law; Elena already decided not to re-trade governing law.

**One real hole.** Clause 6 says "Company" means Nishant personally *"unless and until the parties execute
a written amendment identifying a different legal entity."* His registration is in progress. When the new
company exists, clients may sign with and pay **the company**, not Nishant. The commission base is money
"received by the Company" — so revenue landing in the new company could fall outside it, and Elena would
need his goodwill to sign a new amendment. That is the exact failure the whole negotiation was built to
prevent: payment depending on goodwill instead of on the page.

**Fix — one sentence (Clause 6A):**

> Any company or other entity that Nishant Chaudhary incorporates, owns or controls to carry on the
> business of IntelliOps Automation is bound by the Agreement and this Addendum from its formation;
> amounts it receives under a qualifying deal are Net Collected Revenue; and Nishant Chaudhary remains
> jointly liable for commission until Elena Revicheva signs a written novation to that entity.

Also ask (small): his **PAN** in the blank he left — it identifies the individual party.
