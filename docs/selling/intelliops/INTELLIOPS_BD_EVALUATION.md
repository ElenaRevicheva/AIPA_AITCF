1. **Net Collected Revenue is a sponge (§6).** Exclusions include taxes,
   refunds, credits, chargebacks, **pass-through amounts, third-party costs,
   reimbursable expenses**. Delivery subcontractors and “pass-through” can
   eat the $25,000 example before 20% is applied.
2. **Verification is open-ended (§7).** “Fifteen business days after
   collection **and verification**” — verification has no clock.
3. **No payment rail.** Panama ← India. Currency, Wise vs wire, who pays
   fees: all missing.
4. **Six-month tail (§19) + 30-day termination (§18).** They can take a
   filled pipeline on 30 days’ notice and keep the deals.
5. **§16 conflicts vs AIdeazz.** Offer B overlaps their services. Without a
   carve-out, originating for them can be read as forbidding her own GEO /
   WhatsApp / AI-ops practice.
6. **They already signed v2.** Countersigning accepts it as-is. Do not.
