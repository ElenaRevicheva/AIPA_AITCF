RECITALS
IntelliOps Automation designs and develops AI-powered RPA, workflow automation and intelligent document
processing solutions around actual business processes.
The Company's published service areas include RPA, AI workflow automation, intelligent document processing,
workflow/API automation, business process automation and automation maintenance.
The Company's published methodology emphasizes process discovery, detailed documentation, right-fit automation
architecture, development/testing, deployment and continuous optimization.
ELENA REVICHEVA is engaged to originate and develop commercial opportunities for these services on a
commission basis.

1. APPOINTMENT AND PURPOSE
IntelliOps Automation appoints ELENA REVICHEVA as an independent Business Development Expert to identify,
introduce, qualify, develop and assist in closing commercial opportunities for IntelliOps Automation. The
engagement is commission-based and does not create a salary, employment or guaranteed-compensation
relationship.

2. COMPANY SERVICES
The BD Expert may promote Company-approved services including AI-powered RPA, AI workflow automation,
intelligent document processing using OCR and related technologies, workflow/API/system integration, business
process automation, and automation maintenance/optimization. The Company's approach is process-first: discovery,
documentation, right-fit architecture, development/testing, deployment and ongoing optimization.

3. BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT DUTIES
The BD Expert shall identify target accounts and decision-makers; conduct lawful professional outreach; qualify
opportunities; arrange discovery calls and demonstrations; support proposals and commercial discussions; maintain
opportunity records; communicate prospect requirements accurately; and coordinate with Company personnel
through contracting and handoff.

4. REPRESENTATION AND AUTHORITY
The BD Expert may not bind IntelliOps Automation or independently approve pricing, discounts, warranties,
guarantees, technical commitments, security representations, delivery dates, refunds, payment terms or contracts.
Final commercial, legal, technical and delivery decisions remain with authorized Company representatives.

5. COMMISSION — 20%
For every Qualifying Deal attributable to the BD Expert, the Company shall pay twenty percent (20%) of Net
Collected Revenue actually received and retained by IntelliOps Automation from that deal. A Qualifying Deal must
result from the BD Expert's documented introduction or material development, be accepted by the Company as
attributable, become a customer transaction, and generate actual customer payment.

6. NET COLLECTED REVENUE
Net Collected Revenue means amounts actually received and retained by the Company, excluding taxes, refunds,
credits, chargebacks, pass-through amounts, third-party costs, reimbursable expenses and amounts not ultimately
collected. Example: $25,000 of qualifying Net Collected Revenue produces a $5,000 commission.

7. PAYMENT OF COMMISSION
Commission is payable only after the Company receives the relevant customer funds. For milestone or installment
billing, commission is calculated proportionally on each collected installment. Approved commission will ordinarily
be paid within fifteen (15) business days after collection and verification.

8. OPPORTUNITY ATTRIBUTION
The BD Expert should register material opportunities by email, CRM entry or other written Company-approved
method. Registration should identify prospect, contact, introduction date, business need and evidence of
involvement. Pre-existing Company accounts, pipeline opportunities or substantially developed prospects are not
commissionable unless the Company confirms eligibility in writing. Where multiple sources contribute, attribution
may be determined in good faith from business records.

9. SALES HANDOFF AND DELIVERY
The Company may perform process discovery, detailed process documentation, automation architecture,
development, testing, deployment, monitoring, maintenance and optimization. The BD Expert shall provide accurate
requirements and facilitate a professional handoff to the Company delivery team.

10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
The BD Expert is an independent contractor and not an employee, officer, partner, shareholder, joint venturer or
legal agent of the Company. The BD Expert is responsible for applicable taxes, registrations, insurance, equipment
and ordinary expenses unless expressly approved by the Company.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY
The BD Expert shall protect non-public Company information, including customer/prospect data, pricing, proposals,
technical information, automation architectures, workflows, credentials, financial information, business plans,
pipeline information and intellectual property. Confidential information shall be used only for authorized Company
business. These obligations survive termination as required by applicable law.

12. DATA PROTECTION AND OUTREACH

marketing requirements, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss or misuse. No
