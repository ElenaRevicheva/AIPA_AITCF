 RECITALS
 IntelliOps Automation designs and develops AI-powered RPA, workflow automation and intelligent document processing
 solutions around actual business processes.

 The Company's published service areas include RPA, AI workflow automation, intelligent document processing,
 workflow/API automation, business process automation and automation maintenance.

 The Company's published methodology emphasizes process discovery, detailed documentation, right-fit automation
 architecture, development/testing, deployment and continuous optimization.

 ELENA REVICHEVA is engaged to originate and develop commercial opportunities for these services on a commission
 basis.

 1. APPOINTMENT AND PURPOSE
 IntelliOps Automation appoints ELENA REVICHEVA as an independent Business Development Expert to identify, introduce,
 qualify, develop and assist in closing commercial opportunities for IntelliOps Automation. The engagement is
 commission-based and does not create a salary, employment or guaranteed-compensation relationship.

 2. COMPANY SERVICES
 The BD Expert may promote Company-approved services including AI-powered RPA, AI workflow automation, intelligent
 document processing using OCR and related technologies, workflow/API/system integration, business process automation,
 and automation maintenance/optimization. The Company's approach is process-first: discovery, documentation, right-fit
 architecture, development/testing, deployment and ongoing optimization.

 3. BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT DUTIES
 The BD Expert shall identify target accounts and decision-makers; conduct lawful professional outreach; qualify
 opportunities; arrange discovery calls and demonstrations; support proposals and commercial discussions; maintain
 opportunity records; communicate prospect requirements accurately; and coordinate with Company personnel through
 contracting and handoff.

 4. REPRESENTATION AND AUTHORITY
 The BD Expert may not bind IntelliOps Automation or independently approve pricing, discounts, warranties, guarantees,
 technical commitments, security representations, delivery dates, refunds, payment terms or contracts. Final commercial,
 legal, technical and delivery decisions remain with authorized Company representatives.

 5. COMMISSION — 20%
 For every Qualifying Deal attributable to the BD Expert, the Company shall pay twenty percent (20%) of Net Collected
 Revenue actually received and retained by IntelliOps Automation from that deal. A Qualifying Deal must result from the BD
 Expert's documented introduction or material development, be accepted by the Company as attributable under Section 8,
 become a customer transaction, and generate actual customer payment.

 6. NET COLLECTED REVENUE
 Net Collected Revenue means amounts actually received and retained by the Company, excluding taxes, refunds, credits,
 chargebacks, pass-through amounts, third-party costs, reimbursable expenses and amounts not ultimately collected.
 Example: $25,000 of qualifying Net Collected Revenue produces a $5,000 commission.

 7. PAYMENT OF COMMISSION
 Commission is payable only after the Company receives the relevant customer funds. For milestone or installment billing,
 commission is calculated proportionally on each collected installment. Approved commission will ordinarily be paid within
 fifteen (15) business days after collection and verification.

 8. OPPORTUNITY ATTRIBUTION — ADVANCE CONFIRMATION
 The BD Expert shall register each material opportunity by email, CRM entry or other written Company-approved method
 before materially investing time in development of the opportunity. Registration shall identify the prospect, relevant contact,
 introduction date, business need and evidence of involvement. Within five (5) business days after receiving a complete
 opportunity registration, the Company shall provide written confirmation that the opportunity is (a) accepted as attributable to
 the BD Expert, (b) rejected as non-attributable, or (c) requires clarification. A rejection shall state the material basis for the
 rejection, including any pre-existing Company account, pipeline opportunity or substantially developed prospect. Once the
 Company confirms an opportunity in writing as attributable, that confirmation shall govern commission eligibility for that
 registered opportunity, subject to the other terms of this Agreement. Where multiple sources contribute to an opportunity,
 the parties shall determine attribution in good faith from business records before the opportunity is materially pursued.

 9. MONTHLY COMMISSION STATEMENT
 The Company shall provide the BD Expert with a written commission statement monthly, covering the preceding month. The
 statement shall identify, for each opportunity attributed to the BD Expert: (i) the customer/deal, (ii) amounts invoiced during
 the period, (iii) amounts actually collected during the period, (iv) applicable deductions from Net Collected Revenue, if any,
 (v) Net Collected Revenue used for commission calculation, and (vi) commission payable at the twenty percent (20%) rate.
 If no amounts were invoiced or collected for an attributed opportunity during the month, the statement may indicate that no
 commission is presently payable for that opportunity.

 10. SALES HANDOFF AND DELIVERY
 The Company may perform process discovery, detailed process documentation, automation architecture, development,
 testing, deployment, monitoring, maintenance and optimization. The BD Expert shall provide accurate requirements and
 facilitate a professional handoff to the Company delivery team.

 11. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
 The BD Expert is an independent contractor and not an employee, officer, partner, shareholder, joint venturer or legal agent
 of the Company. The BD Expert is responsible for applicable taxes, registrations, insurance, equipment and ordinary
 expenses unless expressly approved by the Company.

 12. CONFIDENTIALITY
 The BD Expert shall protect non-public Company information, including customer/prospect data, pricing, proposals,
 technical information, automation architectures, workflows, credentials, financial information, business plans, pipeline
 information and intellectual property. Confidential information shall be used only for authorized Company business. These
 obligations survive termination as required by applicable law.

 13. DATA PROTECTION AND OUTREACH
 The BD Expert shall use prospect and customer data responsibly, comply with applicable privacy and electronic-marketing
 requirements, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss or misuse. No deceptive, fraudulent,
 unlawful or misleading outreach is permitted.

 14. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND BRAND
 All IntelliOps Automation branding, trademarks, website content, proposals, presentations, software, workflows,
 documentation, automation designs and other Company intellectual property remain Company property or the property of its
 licensors. The BD Expert receives only a limited right to use approved materials for authorized business development.

 During the term and for twelve (12) months after termination, the BD Expert shall not knowingly divert, conceal, circumvent

 related business outside IntelliOps Automation.

 16. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
 The BD Expert shall disclose material conflicts and shall not use Company confidential information, prospects, pricing,
 proposals or relationships for another business or competing engagement.

 17. EXPENSES
 Expenses are reimbursable only when approved by the Company in writing before being incurred and supported by
 reasonable documentation.

 18. TERM AND TERMINATION
 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate upon thirty (30)
 days' written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for fraud, material breach, unauthorized commitments,
 misuse of confidential information, unlawful conduct or serious misconduct.

 19. POST-TERMINATION COMMISSION
 Termination does not extinguish commissions already earned. A documented qualifying opportunity confirmed in writing as
 attributable to the BD Expert before termination remains commission-eligible if the resulting customer transaction is
 executed within six (6) months after termination and the Company receives the relevant Net Collected Revenue.

 20. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
 The BD Expert shall not make false or misleading statements, promise unauthorized results, misrepresent Company
 capabilities, offer unauthorized discounts, guarantee savings or performance, impersonate Company personnel, or engage
 in bribery, fraud or unlawful activity.

 21. INDEMNIFICATION
 Each party shall be responsible for losses caused by its own fraud, willful misconduct, material breach or violation of
 applicable law, subject to applicable legal limitations.

 22. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, without regard to conflict-of-law
 principles. The parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct discussion. If the dispute cannot
 be resolved amicably, the courts having competent jurisdiction in New Delhi, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction, subject
 to any mandatory provisions of applicable law.

 23. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
 This Agreement constitutes the complete agreement concerning the engagement and supersedes prior discussions on the
 same subject. Amendments or waivers must be in writing and accepted by both parties.

 24. ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES

 platform, to the extent permitted by applicable law. Electronic execution evidences each party's intent to be bound.

 Each party confirms that it has read and understood this Agreement, has had an opportunity to seek independent
 professional advice, and voluntarily agrees to its terms.
